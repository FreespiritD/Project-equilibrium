# EQUILIBRIUM Implementation Timeline Template for Medical Schools

**Customizable year-by-year implementation plan with milestones and deliverables**

---

## Purpose

This template provides a structured timeline for implementing Project EQUILIBRIUM curriculum at your medical school. Adapt timelines, milestones, and activities to your local context, resources, and implementation approach (full integration vs modular pilot).

**Three Implementation Models Included:**
1. **Full Curriculum Integration** (2-3 years)
2. **Modular Pilot** (6-12 months)
3. **Accelerated Integration** (1-2 years for well-resourced schools)

---

## Template Instructions

1. Choose the model that best fits your readiness level and resources
2. Customize dates, milestones, and deliverables to your context
3. Assign responsibilities to specific individuals or committees
4. Track progress using the milestone checkboxes
5. Adjust timeline as needed based on evaluation data

---

# Model 1: Full Curriculum Integration Timeline

**Best for:** Schools with high readiness, leadership support, adequate resources
**Timeline:** 24-36 months from planning to full implementation

---

## Year 0: Preparation Phase (Months 1-6)

### Month 1-2: Foundation and Buy-In

**Milestone 1.1: Secure Leadership Approval**
- [ ] Present EQUILIBRIUM proposal to curriculum committee
- [ ] Present to senior leadership (Dean, Head of Medical Education)
- [ ] Secure formal approval to proceed with planning
- [ ] Allocate initial planning resources

**Deliverable:** Signed approval/memorandum of understanding

**Milestone 1.2: Establish Working Group**
- [ ] Recruit 6-10 faculty members (psychiatry, GP, public health, lifestyle medicine)
- [ ] Include student representative
- [ ] Appoint working group chair/lead
- [ ] Schedule regular meetings (monthly minimum)

**Deliverable:** Working group terms of reference, meeting schedule

**Milestone 1.3: Baseline Data Collection**
- [ ] Survey current mental health curriculum content (hours, topics, by year)
- [ ] Survey faculty attitudes and expertise
- [ ] Survey student knowledge baseline (optional: pre-implementation test)
- [ ] Review GMC outcomes alignment

**Deliverable:** Baseline curriculum map and gap analysis

---

### Month 3-4: Detailed Planning

**Milestone 1.4: Customize Curriculum**
- [ ] Review EQUILIBRIUM curriculum materials (all years)
- [ ] Map to local curriculum structure
- [ ] Identify integration points across years
- [ ] Determine what content to add, modify, or remove
- [ ] Draft year-by-year content outline

**Deliverable:** EQUILIBRIUM curriculum integration plan (20-30 pages)

**Milestone 1.5: Faculty Development Plan**
- [ ] Identify faculty training needs
- [ ] Schedule 2-day EQUILIBRIUM faculty training course
- [ ] Identify faculty champions for each year
- [ ] Plan ongoing faculty support

**Deliverable:** Faculty development program outline

**Milestone 1.6: Assessment Strategy**
- [ ] Design formative assessments (quizzes, reflections)
- [ ] Design summative assessments (OSCEs, written exams)
- [ ] Plan for competency evaluation
- [ ] Integrate into existing assessment framework

**Deliverable:** Assessment blueprint

---

### Month 5-6: Pilot Preparation

**Milestone 1.7: Select Pilot Cohort**
- [ ] Choose pilot year group (typically Year 1 or Year 2)
- [ ] Confirm pilot start date
- [ ] Communicate with students
- [ ] Adjust timetable for pilot cohort

**Deliverable:** Pilot cohort identified, timetable adjusted

**Milestone 1.8: Materials Development**
- [ ] Adapt EQUILIBRIUM lecture slides for local context
- [ ] Prepare case studies relevant to local population
- [ ] Set up online learning resources (LMS)
- [ ] Print student handouts/workbooks

**Deliverable:** Complete teaching materials for pilot year

**Milestone 1.9: Secure Clinical Placements**
- [ ] Identify 3-5 GP practices supportive of holistic approach
- [ ] Brief clinical supervisors on EQUILIBRIUM
- [ ] Arrange student observation/experiential learning opportunities

**Deliverable:** Clinical placement agreements

**Milestone 1.10: Evaluation Plan**
- [ ] Define evaluation questions
- [ ] Choose evaluation methods (surveys, focus groups, exams, OSCE scores)
- [ ] Set success metrics
- [ ] Secure ethics approval if research component

**Deliverable:** Evaluation protocol

---

## Year 1: Pilot Phase (Months 7-18)

### Month 7-9: Faculty Training and Launch

**Milestone 2.1: Faculty Training**
- [ ] Deliver 2-day EQUILIBRIUM faculty intensive
- [ ] Provide teaching materials and facilitator guides
- [ ] Schedule follow-up faculty support sessions
- [ ] Establish faculty learning community

**Deliverable:** Trained faculty cohort

**Milestone 2.2: Pilot Launch**
- [ ] Deliver Year 1 content (biopsychosocial foundations, evidence base)
- [ ] OR Year 2 content (skills in MI, CBT basics, lifestyle medicine)
- [ ] Monitor student engagement and feedback
- [ ] Troubleshoot issues in real-time

**Deliverable:** Pilot teaching delivered

---

### Month 10-12: Midpoint Evaluation

**Milestone 2.3: Formative Evaluation**
- [ ] Student feedback surveys (mid-year)
- [ ] Faculty debrief meetings
- [ ] Review formative assessment results
- [ ] Identify what's working and what needs adjustment

**Deliverable:** Midpoint evaluation report with recommendations

**Milestone 2.4: Adjustments**
- [ ] Modify teaching based on feedback
- [ ] Provide additional faculty support where needed
- [ ] Update materials

---

### Month 13-18: Complete Pilot Year

**Milestone 2.5: Complete Pilot Delivery**
- [ ] Deliver all planned EQUILIBRIUM content for pilot year
- [ ] Administer summative assessments (OSCEs, exams)
- [ ] Collect final student feedback

**Deliverable:** Pilot year complete

**Milestone 2.6: Comprehensive Evaluation**
- [ ] Analyze student knowledge/skills (pre-post comparison)
- [ ] Analyze exam and OSCE performance
- [ ] Student focus groups
- [ ] Faculty focus groups
- [ ] Compare outcomes to previous cohorts (if data available)

**Deliverable:** Pilot evaluation report (30-40 pages)

**Milestone 2.7: Decision to Scale**
- [ ] Present evaluation to curriculum committee
- [ ] Present to senior leadership
- [ ] Secure approval to expand to additional years
- [ ] Refine based on pilot learning

**Deliverable:** Approval to expand implementation

---

## Year 2: Expansion Phase (Months 19-30)

### Month 19-24: Expand to Additional Years

**Milestone 3.1: Expand to Two Year Groups**
- [ ] Continue with pilot cohort (now Year 2 or 3)
- [ ] Begin with new Year 1 cohort
- [ ] Deliver refined curriculum based on pilot learning
- [ ] Train additional faculty as needed

**Deliverable:** EQUILIBRIUM in 2 year groups

**Milestone 3.2: Develop Advanced Content**
- [ ] Prepare Years 3-4 clinical content
- [ ] Develop clinical reasoning scenarios
- [ ] Prepare advanced OSCE stations (biopsychosocial assessment, SDM)
- [ ] Coordinate with clinical placement sites

**Deliverable:** Years 3-4 curriculum materials ready

---

### Month 25-30: Continue Expansion

**Milestone 3.3: Expand to Third Year Group**
- [ ] Add Year 3 content (clinical application)
- [ ] EQUILIBRIUM now in Years 1, 2, and 3
- [ ] Ongoing faculty development and support

**Deliverable:** EQUILIBRIUM in 3 year groups

**Milestone 3.4: Preliminary Outcome Data**
- [ ] Collect data on pilot cohort's clinical performance
- [ ] Analyze OSCE pass rates
- [ ] Survey clinical supervisors on student competence
- [ ] Track student confidence and satisfaction

**Deliverable:** Preliminary outcomes report

---

## Year 3: Full Integration and Sustainability (Months 31-36+)

### Month 31-36: Complete Vertical Integration

**Milestone 4.1: Full Implementation**
- [ ] EQUILIBRIUM integrated across all 4 (or 5) years
- [ ] All students receiving EQUILIBRIUM curriculum
- [ ] Regular faculty development ongoing
- [ ] Assessment fully integrated

**Deliverable:** Full vertical integration achieved

**Milestone 4.2: Sustainability Planning**
- [ ] Establish ongoing faculty learning community
- [ ] Create faculty teaching resources repository
- [ ] Plan annual curriculum review cycle
- [ ] Embed in standard curriculum management processes

**Deliverable:** Sustainability plan

**Milestone 4.3: Outcomes Evaluation**
- [ ] First cohort to complete full 4-5 year EQUILIBRIUM curriculum graduates
- [ ] Collect graduate outcomes:
  - Knowledge/skills assessment
  - Confidence in holistic mental health care
  - Prescribing behavior in F1/F2 placements
- [ ] Compare to pre-EQUILIBRIUM graduates
- [ ] Patient outcomes in student placements (if feasible)

**Deliverable:** Comprehensive outcomes evaluation (50-100 pages)

**Milestone 4.4: Dissemination**
- [ ] Present at medical education conferences
- [ ] Publish implementation case study
- [ ] Share learning with other medical schools
- [ ] Contribute to EQUILIBRIUM community of practice

**Deliverable:** Conference presentations, publications

---

## Beyond Year 3: Continuous Improvement

- Annual curriculum review and updates
- Ongoing faculty development (onboarding new faculty, refresher training)
- Ongoing evaluation and quality improvement
- Adaptation based on emerging evidence
- Sharing best practices nationally/internationally

# Model 2: Modular Pilot Timeline

**Best for:** Schools with moderate readiness, testing EQUILIBRIUM before full integration
**Timeline:** 6-12 months for pilot, then 1-2 years for expansion if successful

---

## Phase 1: Planning (Months 1-2)

**Milestone: Secure Approval and Plan**
- [ ] Present modular pilot proposal (integrate into existing module, e.g., psychiatry)
- [ ] Identify pilot module and year group
- [ ] Establish small working group (3-4 faculty)
- [ ] Customize EQUILIBRIUM content for this module (10-20 hours of teaching)
- [ ] Plan evaluation

**Deliverable:** Pilot plan approved, materials ready

---

## Phase 2: Faculty Training (Month 2-3)

**Milestone: Train Faculty**
- [ ] 1-day EQUILIBRIUM faculty workshop (abbreviated from 2-day)
- [ ] Provide teaching materials and facilitator guides
- [ ] Faculty practice delivering content

**Deliverable:** Faculty ready to teach

---

## Phase 3: Pilot Delivery (Months 3-6)

**Milestone: Deliver Modular Pilot**
- [ ] Integrate 10-20 hours of EQUILIBRIUM content into chosen module
- [ ] Collect student feedback ongoing
- [ ] Assess student knowledge/skills
- [ ] Faculty debrief

**Deliverable:** Pilot delivered

---

## Phase 4: Evaluation and Decision (Months 7-8)

**Milestone: Evaluate and Decide Next Steps**
- [ ] Comprehensive evaluation (student outcomes, feedback, faculty experience)
- [ ] Present to curriculum committee and leadership
- [ ] Decision: Expand to full integration OR continue modular approach OR discontinue
- [ ] If expanding, follow Model 1 timeline starting at Year 1

**Deliverable:** Evaluation report, decision on next steps

---

# Model 3: Accelerated Integration Timeline

**Best for:** Schools with very high readiness, adequate resources, streamlined governance
**Timeline:** 18-24 months from planning to full implementation

Uses similar milestones to Model 1 but:
- Preparation phase: 3 months (vs 6)
- Pilot 2 year groups simultaneously (Year 1 and Year 2)
- Expansion to all years within 12 months
- More intensive faculty training (1 week vs 2 days)
- Requires significant resource commitment

*See Model 1 for detailed milestones, but compress timeline*

# Risk Management and Contingency Planning

Identify potential risks and mitigation strategies:

| Risk | Likelihood (H/M/L) | Impact (H/M/L) | Mitigation Strategy | Contingency Plan |
|------|-------------------|----------------|---------------------|------------------|
| Faculty resistance | | | Present evidence, start with champions, demonstrate student outcomes | Proceed with smaller pilot using only supportive faculty |
| Resource constraints | | | Seek grant funding, use existing teaching slots, phased approach | Scale back to modular pilot |
| Leadership change | | | Document decisions, embed in curriculum, broad faculty ownership | Ensure multiple champions, not dependent on single leader |
| Student timetable overcrowded | | | Integrate (don't add), replace low-value content | Flipped classroom, online modules |
| Clinical placements don't model approach | | | Select supportive sites, train supervisors, use simulated patients for assessment | Assessment via OSCEs, student reflection on placement |

*Add risks specific to your context*

# Resource Planning

## Faculty Time Required

**Preparation Phase:**
- Lead: 0.3-0.5 FTE for 6 months
- Working group: 0.1 FTE each × 6-10 members
- **Total:** ~1.5-2 FTE for 6 months

**Pilot Delivery:**
- Teaching: 40 hours = 0.02 FTE per year group
- Coordination: 0.1 FTE
- **Total:** ~0.15-0.2 FTE ongoing per year

**Faculty Development:**
- Initial training: 2 days × 20 faculty = 40 faculty days
- Ongoing: 4 hours/year per faculty

**Total Faculty Time (Years 1-3):** ~3-4 FTE

## Financial Resources

**Estimated Costs:**
- Faculty development: £5,000-10,000 (training delivery, materials)
- Materials development/adaptation: £3,000-5,000
- Educational technology: £2,000-5,000 (LMS, online modules)
- Evaluation: £5,000-10,000 (if robust evaluation with external input)
- Backfill for faculty time: £50,000-100,000 (if needed)

**Total Estimated Cost:** £65,000-130,000 over 3 years

*Many schools implement with <£20,000 by using existing resources creatively*

# Success Metrics and Evaluation Timeline

## Formative Evaluation (Ongoing)

- **Monthly:** Faculty debrief meetings
- **End of each term:** Student feedback surveys
- **End of each year:** Review formative assessment data

## Summative Evaluation

- **After Pilot (Year 1):** Comprehensive pilot evaluation
- **After 2 years:** Expansion outcomes
- **After 3 years:** Full implementation outcomes including first graduate cohort

## Key Metrics to Track

**Student Learning:**
- Knowledge scores (pre-post tests)
- OSCE performance (biopsychosocial assessment, SDM stations)
- Student confidence ratings
- Student satisfaction

**Faculty:**
- Faculty confidence and satisfaction
- Faculty engagement and retention

**Implementation Quality:**
- Fidelity to EQUILIBRIUM curriculum
- Coverage of planned content
- Assessment completion rates

**Downstream Outcomes (if feasible):**
- Graduate prescribing patterns in F1/F2
- Graduate confidence in managing depression holistically
- Patient outcomes in student placements
