# Medical School Pilot Application

## About the Pilot Program

Project EQUILIBRIUM is seeking 5 medical schools to participate in the pilot phase (2026-2028) of our comprehensive mental health education transformation.

## Why Become a Pilot Site?

### Benefits

**For Your Institution:**
- Position as leader in medical education innovation
- Access to evidence-based curriculum materials
- Comprehensive faculty training and support
- Participation in multi-site research
- Financial support for implementation
- Networking with peer institutions

**For Your Students:**
- Comprehensive mental health competency
- Evidence-based holistic intervention skills
- Better preparation for modern practice
- Enhanced career readiness

**For Your Faculty:**
- Professional development opportunities
- Access to teaching resources
- Peer learning community
- Contribution to medical education advancement

### Support Provided

- **Curriculum materials**: Complete package for Years 1-4
- **Faculty training**: 2-day workshops and ongoing support
- **Implementation consultant**: Dedicated support throughout
- **Peer network**: Connection with other pilot schools
- **Financial support**: £20-50K depending on institution size
- **Research support**: Evaluation and data collection assistance

## Selection Criteria

We will select 5 schools based on:

### Essential Criteria

✓ **Leadership commitment**: Dean/curriculum lead endorsement
✓ **Faculty champions**: At least 2 engaged faculty members
✓ **Curriculum space**: Ability to integrate content
✓ **Timeline readiness**: Able to start September 2026
✓ **Evaluation participation**: Commitment to data collection
✓ **2-year commitment**: Minimum pilot duration

### Desirable Criteria

- Innovation track record
- Diverse student body
- Geographic representation
- Different curriculum models (PBL, traditional, integrated)
- Established mental health teaching
- Research capacity
- Enthusiasm and vision for transformation

### Timeline

- **June 2025**: Applications open

**Step 1: Expression of Interest (EOI)**

**Step 2: Full Application**
- Detailed application (invited based on EOI)

**Expression of Interest:**
- Institution name and key contact

   - Institutional contribution

## Selection Process

### Evaluation

Applications will be evaluated on:
- **Strategic fit**: Alignment with EQUILIBRIUM goals (25%)
- **Readiness**: Capacity and commitment to implement (25%)
- **Quality of plan**: Feasibility and thoroughness (20%)
- **Leadership support**: Strength of institutional commitment (15%)
- **Diversity**: Geographic and institutional diversity of cohort (10%)
- **Innovation potential**: Likelihood of generating insights (5%)

### Decision

- **October 2025**: Selection committee review
- **Mid-October**: Site visits or video interviews (finalists)
- **Late October**: Final decisions
- **Early November**: Notification and partnership agreements

### Not Selected?

If not selected for pilot:
- Feedback provided
- Opportunity to join scale-up phase (2027-2029)
- Access to curriculum materials when available
- Invitation to learning community

## What to Expect as a Pilot Site

### Preparation Phase (Nov 2025 - Aug 2026)

- Partnership agreement finalized
- Detailed implementation planning
- Curriculum mapping and integration
- Faculty training scheduled
- Systems and resources prepared
- Baseline data collection

### Implementation Phase (Sep 2026 - Aug 2028)

**Year 1 (2026-2027):**
- First cohort begins EQUILIBRIUM curriculum
- Regular support calls with implementation team
- Peer learning community meetings (monthly)
- Ongoing faculty development
- Process evaluation and rapid feedback
- Mid-year review and refinement

**Year 2 (2027-2028):**
- Second cohort begins
- First cohort progresses to Year 2 content
- Continued support and monitoring
- Outcome data collection
- End-of-year comprehensive evaluation
- Dissemination of findings

### Time Commitment

**Institutional:**
- Implementation planning: 20-30 hours (preparation phase)
- Curriculum development: 40-60 hours (adapting materials)
- Ongoing coordination: 5-10 hours/month

**Faculty:**
- Training: 2 days initial + 1 day annual refresher
- Curriculum delivery: Integrated into existing teaching
- Peer learning: 1 hour/month (optional)

**Students:**
- Additional curriculum time: ~10 hours/year
- Evaluation participation: 2-3 hours/year
