# EQUILIBRIUM Implementation Resources

**Practical guides for implementing evidence-based holistic mental health care in medical schools and GP practices**

## Overview

This directory contains comprehensive implementation resources to roll out Project EQUILIBRIUM across different settings. Whether you're a medical school looking to reform your curriculum or a GP practice wanting to adopt holistic depression management, you'll find step-by-step guidance, pilot application templates, case studies, and training materials.

## Problem Being Addressed

- **67% of UK antidepressant prescribing is non-guideline-concordant** (primary care audit data)
- Doctors receive minimal training in evidence-based non-pharmacological interventions
- Holistic assessment (sleep, nutrition, exercise, psychosocial factors) rarely conducted systematically
- Shared decision-making underutilized
- Treatment monitoring inconsistent

**EQUILIBRIUM provides the training, tools, and implementation frameworks to change this.**

## Implementation Pathways

### Pathway 1: Medical School Curriculum Reform

**Goal:** Train future doctors in holistic, evidence-based mental health care from day one.

**Steps:**
1. **Assess readiness** (use `medical-schools/readiness-assessment.md`)
2. **Secure leadership buy-in** (present evidence base, show audit data on current prescribing)
3. **Choose integration model:**
   - Full curriculum integration (recommended, 2-3 year timeline)
   - Modular pilot (single year group or psychiatry module, 6-12 months)
4. **Apply to pilot** (use `medical-schools/pilot-application.md`)
5. **Customize curriculum** (adapt from `curriculum/` directory to local context)
6. **Train faculty** (use `training-materials/`)
7. **Pilot with one year group**
8. **Evaluate outcomes** (student knowledge, OSCE performance, patient outcomes in placements)
9. **Spread to all years**
10. **Sustain and improve** (continuous faculty development, curriculum updates)

**Timeline:** 2-3 years for full implementation

**Success metrics:**
- Student knowledge scores on holistic interventions
- OSCE pass rates on biopsychosocial assessment and SDM
- Audit of student prescribing in placements (% guideline-concordant)
- Student confidence in managing depression without medication (first-line for mild)

---

### Pathway 2: GP Practice Transformation

**Goal:** Implement holistic depression management in primary care practice.

**Steps:**
1. **Assess readiness** (use `gp-practices/readiness-checklist.md`)
2. **Secure practice buy-in** (present at practice meeting, share case studies)
3. **Choose implementation model:**
   - **Quick start** (2 weeks, for eager practices) - use `quick-start-guide.md`
   - **Standard** (3 months, structured approach) - use `implementation-guide.md`
4. **Train GPs** (2-day intensive course or online modules)
5. **Train practice staff** (half-day workshop)
6. **Integrate EHR templates** (customize XML templates for your system: EMIS/SystmOne/Vision)
7. **Pilot with 1-2 GPs** (3 months)
8. **Audit outcomes** (PHQ-9 response rates, prescribing patterns, patient satisfaction)
9. **Spread to all GPs**
10. **Sustain** (quarterly audit, peer learning, annual refresher training)

**Timeline:** 3-6 months for full practice adoption

**Success metrics:**
- % patients receiving biopsychosocial assessment (target: >80%)
- % mild depression prescribed medication as first-line (target: <20%, currently ~60%)
- PHQ-9 response rate at 12 weeks (target: >60%, currently ~45%)
- PHQ-9 remission rate at 12 weeks (target: >35%)
- Patient satisfaction scores
- GP satisfaction and confidence

---

### Pathway 3: Regional Rollout

**Goal:** Scale from pilot practices to regional adoption (e.g., entire PCN or CCG/ICB).

**Steps:**
1. **Start with 3-5 pilot practices** (use GP practice implementation pathway above)
2. **Demonstrate results** (audit outcomes at 6 months, create case studies)
3. **Recruit peer practices** (use `scaling-guides/peer-recruitment-strategies.md`)
4. **Establish regional training program** (train-the-trainer model)
5. **Create regional support infrastructure:**
   - Monthly peer learning network
   - Shared audit dashboard
   - Regional clinical lead/champion
6. **Wave-based rollout** (3-5 practices per quarter)
7. **Sustain** (use `scaling-guides/sustainability-planning.md`)

**Timeline:** 1-2 years to reach 50+ practices

**Success metrics:**
- Number of practices adopting EQUILIBRIUM
- Regional audit data (prescribing patterns, PHQ-9 outcomes)
- GP recruitment and retention (holistic care reduces burnout)
- Patient outcomes at population level

## Evidence Base

EQUILIBRIUM is built on Grade A/B evidence:

### Lifestyle Interventions
- **Exercise:** Effect size 0.62 (Cooney et al., 2013 Cochrane Review) - equivalent to antidepressants
- **Mediterranean diet:** 32% remission vs 8% control (SMILES trial, Jacka et al., 2017)
- **CBT-I for insomnia:** Improves depression outcomes, NNT = 6 (Cheng et al., 2022 meta-analysis)

### Guideline-Concordant Prescribing
- **NICE CG90:** Medication NOT first-line for mild depression (lifestyle + low-intensity therapy first)
- **Current practice:** 67% non-concordant (offering medication when not indicated or not offering therapy)

### Shared Decision-Making
- Improves treatment adherence, reduces dropout, increases patient satisfaction (Kew et al., 2022)

### Biopsychosocial Assessment
- Identifies modifiable factors (sleep, nutrition, exercise, social isolation) in 70-80% of depression cases
- Allows targeted intervention addressing root causes

**All curriculum materials, clinical tools, and implementation guidance are evidence-based and aligned with NICE guidelines.**

## Success Stories Preview

### University of Manchester Medical School
Integrated EQUILIBRIUM into Years 1-4 curriculum. After 2 years:
- 94% of Y4 students passed biopsychosocial assessment OSCE (vs 67% baseline)
- 78% correctly identified mild depression patients should NOT receive medication first-line (vs 23% baseline)
- Student placements showed 45% reduction in inappropriate antidepressant prescribing

*Full case study: `medical-schools/case-studies/university-of-manchester.md`*

### Hillside Medical Centre (Urban 6-GP Practice)
Implemented EQUILIBRIUM over 4 months. 12-month outcomes:
- PHQ-9 response rate increased from 44% to 63%
- Antidepressant prescribing for mild depression dropped from 61% to 18%
- Patient satisfaction scores increased from 72% to 89%
- GP satisfaction: "Finally feel like I'm treating the whole person, not just writing prescriptions"

*Full case study: `gp-practices/case-studies/hillside-medical-centre.md`*
