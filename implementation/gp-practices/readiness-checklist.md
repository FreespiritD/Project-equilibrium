# EQUILIBRIUM Readiness Checklist for GP Practices

**50-point checklist to assess your practice's readiness to implement holistic depression management**

## Purpose

This checklist helps GP practices:
- Assess current readiness for EQUILIBRIUM implementation
- Identify strengths to leverage
- Pinpoint gaps to address before starting
- Choose appropriate implementation timeline (quick-start 2 weeks, standard 3 months, or preparation needed)
- Estimate resources required

**Instructions:** Check each box if the statement is true for your practice. Count your total score.

---

## Interpreting Your Score

### 40-50 points: HIGH READINESS - Quick-Start Possible ✅

**Interpretation:** Your practice is ready to implement EQUILIBRIUM now.

**Recommended approach:** **Quick-Start (2 weeks)** or Standard (3 months)

**Timeline:**
- Week 1: GP training, EHR setup, staff briefing
- Week 2: Launch pilot with 1-2 GPs
- Weeks 3-8: Expand to all GPs
- Months 3-6: Refine and sustain

**Next steps:**
1. Secure final partner approval and resource allocation
2. Book GP training (2-day intensive or online modules)
3. Identify 1-2 pilot GPs
4. Use **quick-start-guide.md** for detailed implementation plan
5. Complete pilot application if joining formal EQUILIBRIUM program

**Success factors you have:**
- Strong leadership and GP engagement
- Good infrastructure (EHR, audit capability)
- Adequate resources
- Culture supportive of change

**Watch out for:**
- Even with high readiness, don't skip training (essential for quality implementation)
- Start with pilot (1-2 GPs) before whole practice (allows learning and refinement)

---

### 30-39 points: MODERATE READINESS - Standard Timeline ⚠️

**Interpretation:** Your practice has good capacity but some gaps to address first.

**Recommended approach:** **Standard Implementation (3 months preparation + 3 months pilot)**

**Timeline:**
- Months 1-3: Address gaps (training, EHR setup, secure resources, build buy-in)
- Month 4: Launch pilot with 1-2 GPs
- Months 5-6: Evaluate pilot, adjust
- Months 7-9: Expand to more GPs
- Months 10-12: Full practice adoption

**Next steps:**
1. Review sections where you scored <50% - prioritize these gaps
2. Common gaps and solutions:
   - **Low GP engagement:** Start with 1-2 enthusiastic GPs, let success spread
   - **Limited resources:** Apply for PCN/ICB quality improvement funding
   - **EHR not set up:** Allocate time for IT lead to customize templates (4-8 hours)
   - **No IAPT relationship:** Contact local IAPT service, establish referral pathway
3. Develop 3-month preparation plan addressing key gaps
4. Use **implementation-guide.md** for detailed standard implementation pathway

**Focus areas based on common gaps:**

**If Section 2 (GPs) scored low:**
- Present evidence base at practice meeting (Cochrane reviews, audit data showing 67% non-concordant prescribing)
- Share case studies (Manchester, Edinburgh, Hillside Medical Centre)
- Start with 1 champion GP, demonstrate success

**If Section 4 (EHR) scored low:**
- Allocate dedicated time for EHR template setup
- Use EQUILIBRIUM templates (available for EMIS/SystmOne/Vision)
- Contact EHR support if needed

**If Section 6 (Resources) scored low:**
- Seek external funding (PCN, ICB innovation funds)
- Start smaller (online training instead of 2-day course initially)
- Use existing clinical meeting time for some training

---

### 20-29 points: DEVELOPING READINESS - Preparation Needed 🔧

**Interpretation:** Significant preparatory work needed before implementation.

**Recommended approach:** **6-12 months preparation → then pilot**

**Timeline:**
- Months 1-6: Build readiness (secure leadership buy-in, train champions, gather resources)
- Months 7-9: Prepare for pilot (EHR setup, training)
- Months 10-12: Small pilot with 1 GP
- Year 2: Evaluate and decide whether to expand

**Next steps:**
1. Identify your biggest barriers (sections scoring 0-1):
   - **Leadership (Section 1):** Build business case, present evidence, show ROI
   - **GP engagement (Section 2):** Identify even 1 interested GP to start
   - **Resources (Section 6):** Seek external funding, start very small
   - **Infrastructure (Section 4):** Invest in EHR capability

2. Start with awareness-raising phase:
   - Grand rounds on inappropriate antidepressant prescribing
   - Practice audit: What % of mild depression patients get medication first-line? (Likely 60-70%)
   - Share patient testimonials (many patients prefer non-medication approaches)
   - Present NICE guidelines (medication NOT first-line for mild depression)

3. Build champion network:
   - Find 1-2 GPs interested in holistic approach
   - Send them to EQUILIBRIUM training
   - Let them pilot informally
   - Share their positive experiences with practice

4. Start small wins:
   - Begin using PHQ-9 routinely (if not already)
   - Start discussing exercise/sleep with some patients
   - Refer more patients to IAPT
   - Track outcomes - build evidence internally

**Focus: Building foundations over 6-12 months, then reassess readiness**

---

### Below 20 points: LOW READINESS - Not Recommended Now ❌

**Interpretation:** Multiple significant barriers exist. EQUILIBRIUM implementation not feasible at this time.

**Recommended approach:** **Focus on building readiness over 1-2 years, then reassess**

**Reality check:**
Without adequate readiness (especially leadership support, GP engagement, and minimum resources), implementation will likely fail and waste time/money.

**Alternative paths:**

1. **Wait for better timing:**
   - New partners join who are more supportive
   - Practice financial situation improves
   - PCN launches quality improvement initiative (opportunity for funding/support)

2. **Individual GP level:**
   - Even if practice not ready, individual GPs can:
     - Attend EQUILIBRIUM training for personal development
     - Adopt holistic approach in their own consultations
     - Use patient handouts and decision aids
     - Informally demonstrate benefits to colleagues

3. **Build case for change:**
   - Conduct practice audit showing current inappropriate prescribing
   - Survey patients about preferences (many want non-medication options)
   - Calculate costs (inappropriate prescribing, treatment failures, repeat appointments)
   - Present data to partners annually until readiness improves

4. **Start somewhere else:**
   - If you're in PCN, maybe another practice in network has higher readiness
   - Let them demonstrate success, then revisit with your practice

**Reassess readiness in 12-24 months**

## Common Gaps and Solutions

### Gap: Only 1 GP interested, others skeptical

**Solution:**
- Start with single GP pilot
- Track their outcomes rigorously (PHQ-9 response rates, patient feedback)
- Share results at practice meeting after 3 months
- Let data convince skeptical GPs
- Success spreads organically

**Example:** Hillside Medical Centre started with 2 GPs. After 3 months, seeing their patient satisfaction scores and outcomes, 4 more GPs requested training.

---

### Gap: No budget for training or implementation

**Solutions:**
1. **Apply for funding:**
   - PCN quality improvement funds
   - ICB transformation funding
   - Educational grants (e.g., Royal College of GPs)
   - Pharmaceutical company educational grants (unrestricted)

2. **Reduce costs:**
   - Use free online EQUILIBRIUM modules instead of 2-day course (reduces cost from £500/GP to £0)
   - Use existing clinical meeting time for some training
   - Phased implementation (1-2 GPs first, then expand - spreads costs)

3. **Demonstrate ROI:**
   - Fewer repeat appointments for depression (patients managed more effectively)
   - Reduced treatment failures (holistic approach better outcomes)
   - Improved patient satisfaction (better ratings, patient retention)
   - GP satisfaction and retention (reduced burnout from "just prescribing")

**Minimal viable budget:** £1,000-2,000 (online training, minimal EHR customization, patient materials)

---

### Gap: 10-minute appointments too short for biopsychosocial assessment

**Solutions:**
1. **Initial assessment longer:** Book 15-20 minute "new depression assessment" appointments
2. **Staged assessment:** Don't assess everything in one visit. First visit: sleep and exercise. Follow-up: diet and social factors.
3. **Practice with EQUILIBRIUM template:** GPs report with practice, biopsychosocial assessment takes 12-15 minutes (only 2-5 minutes more than standard)
4. **Reframe:** Thorough assessment upfront saves time later (fewer repeat appointments, better treatment choices, fewer treatment failures)

**Quote from Hillside GP:** "I thought it would take too long. After 2 weeks of practice, I can do comprehensive biopsychosocial assessment in 12 minutes. It's become second nature."

---

### Gap: Poor relationship with IAPT, long waiting lists

**Solutions:**
1. **Rebuild IAPT relationship:** Arrange meeting with IAPT lead, discuss collaboration
2. **Use low-intensity options:** Online CBT (e.g., Beating the Blues, SilverCloud), guided self-help (doesn't require IAPT capacity)
3. **Lifestyle interventions don't require IAPT:** Exercise, diet, sleep can be prescribed by GP
4. **Social prescribing:** Many PCNs now have social prescribing link workers
5. **Private therapy:** Some patients willing to pay, provide list of local therapists

**EQUILIBRIUM doesn't depend on IAPT** - multiple treatment options available

---

### Gap: EHR system not EMIS/SystmOne/Vision

**Solutions:**
1. **Adapt templates:** EQUILIBRIUM XML templates can be adapted for other systems
2. **Paper-based initially:** Use paper versions of biopsychosocial assessment template, transfer key data to EHR
3. **Request EHR vendor support:** Contact your EHR vendor, they may help customize
4. **Focus on approach, not templates:** Even without perfect EHR integration, can implement holistic approach

**Templates are helpful but not essential** - the approach is what matters

## Need Help?

**If your readiness assessment reveals significant gaps:**

1. **Review case studies** - Hillside Medical Centre and City Practice Leeds show how practices overcame barriers
2. **Connect with practices that have implemented** - Learn from their experience
3. **Start smaller** - Even single GP pilot can demonstrate value
4. **Seek external support** - PCN, ICB, or EQUILIBRIUM program team

**Remember:** Moderate readiness is sufficient to start. Don't wait for perfect conditions.

The key is honest assessment of where you are, and a plan to address gaps.
