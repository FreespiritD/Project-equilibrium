# EQUILIBRIUM Quick-Start Guide for GP Practices

**Implement holistic depression management in 2 weeks - For high-readiness practices**

## Who Is This For?

This quick-start guide is for GP practices that:

✅ **Scored 40-50 on readiness checklist** (high readiness)
✅ **Have strong leadership buy-in** (partners approved, resources allocated)
✅ **Have 2+ enthusiastic GPs** ready to pilot
✅ **Can dedicate time NOW** (training this week, launch next week)
✅ **Want to move fast** and iterate as you go

**Not for you?** If readiness score <40, use **implementation-guide.md** for standard 3-6 month timeline.

---

## Prerequisites Check

Before starting quick-start, ensure you have:

- [ ] **Leadership approval** - Partners agreed to proceed
- [ ] **Budget allocated** - £2,000-5,000 for training and setup
- [ ] **Pilot GPs identified** - 2-3 GPs committed to pilot
- [ ] **Protected time** - 2 days for GP training (or time for online modules)
- [ ] **Practice manager support** - Someone coordinating implementation
- [ ] **EHR access** - Someone who can customize templates (GP with admin rights, IT lead, or practice manager)

**All checked?** You're ready for quick-start! **Missing items?** Address these first (may take 1-2 weeks).

---

## Quick-Start Timeline Overview

| Week | Focus | Key Activities | Outcome |
|------|-------|----------------|---------|
| **Week 1** | Preparation | GP training, staff briefing, EHR setup | Ready to pilot |
| **Week 2** | Pilot Launch | 1-2 GPs see patients using EQUILIBRIUM | Learning and refining |
| **Weeks 3-4** | Refinement | Adjust based on pilot, train more GPs | Preparing to scale |
| **Weeks 5-8** | Expansion | More GPs adopt, spread across practice | Whole practice adopting |
| **Month 3** | Evaluation | Audit outcomes, celebrate successes | Decide on sustainment |

**Total time to whole-practice adoption: 6-8 weeks**

# Week 1: Intensive Preparation

## Day 1 (Monday): Kickoff and Planning

### Morning (9am-12pm): Leadership Alignment

**Partners Meeting (30 min)**
- Final approval and commitment
- Confirm resources allocated
- Agree on pilot GPs and timeline
- Appoint implementation lead (usually practice manager or lead GP)

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Partners sign off on £2,000-5,000 budget
- [ ] 2-3 pilot GPs confirmed (names: _______________)
- [ ] Implementation lead appointed (name: _______________)
- [ ] Timeline agreed (quick-start 2 weeks)

**Practice Manager Tasks (2 hours)**
- Book GP training (2-day intensive OR online modules starting today)
- Book half-day staff training (Day 4 or 5 this week)
- Order/print patient materials (EQUILIBRIUM patient guides)
- Set up project folder (digital and physical) for resources

**Checklist:**
- [ ] GP training booked (date: _______, format: in-person / online)
- [ ] Staff training scheduled (date: ________)
- [ ] Patient materials ordered/printed (5 guides × 50 copies each)
- [ ] Project folder created with all EQUILIBRIUM resources

---

### Afternoon (1-5pm): Pilot GP Preparation

**Pilot GPs Meeting (1 hour)**
- Review EQUILIBRIUM overview and evidence base
- Discuss roles (who sees which patients during pilot)
- Set expectations (learning curve first week, perfection not expected)
- Plan patient selection for pilot week

**Begin Online Training (If using online modules)** (3 hours)
- Module 1: Evidence base for holistic interventions (30 min)
- Module 2: Biopsychosocial assessment (1 hour)
- Module 3: Lifestyle interventions as treatment (1 hour)
- Module 4: Shared decision-making (30 min)

**Alternatively: Travel to 2-day intensive course** (if in-person training chosen)

---

## Day 2 (Tuesday): GP Training Continues

### If In-Person Training: Day 1 of 2-Day Intensive
- Attend full-day training course (9am-5pm)
- Topics: Evidence base, biopsychosocial assessment, lifestyle interventions
- Practice skills with role-plays and case studies

### If Online Training: Complete Remaining Modules
- Module 5: Treatment planning and monitoring (1 hour)
- Module 6: Deprescribing safely (1 hour)
- Review case studies (2 hours)
- Practice with EQUILIBRIUM templates (2 hours)

**All GPs (evening):** Review biopsychosocial assessment template, practice mentally walking through it

---

## Day 3 (Wednesday): GP Training Completion + EHR Setup

### Morning: Complete Training

**If In-Person:** Day 2 of 2-Day Intensive
- Attend full-day training (9am-5pm)
- Topics: Treatment planning, monitoring, deprescribing, complex cases
- Return to practice by 6pm

**If Online:** Final Preparation
- Complete any remaining training modules
- Watch video demonstrations of skills
- Complete knowledge check quizzes
- Certificate of completion

---

### Afternoon: EHR Template Setup (Parallel with Training)

**IT Lead / Practice Manager with EHR Admin Access** (4 hours)

**Task: Install EQUILIBRIUM EHR Templates**

1. **Download EQUILIBRIUM Templates** (30 min)
   - Download XML templates from EQUILIBRIUM resources
   - Templates: biopsychosocial-assessment.xml, treatment-plan-template.xml, monitoring-schedule.xml

2. **Adapt for Your EHR System** (1 hour)
   - EMIS: Import into template manager
   - SystmOne: Import via Clinical Template Manager
   - Vision: Create custom form
   - See integration guidance in each XML file

3. **Customize for Local Context** (1 hour)
   - Add local IAPT referral pathway details
   - Add local social prescribing link worker contact
   - Add local exercise referral options (if available)
   - Add practice-specific patient information

4. **Test Templates** (1 hour)
   - Create test patient (or use training record)
   - Complete biopsychosocial assessment template
   - Complete treatment plan template
   - Ensure data saves correctly, calculations work
   - Test that templates link together

5. **Train Pilot GPs on Templates** (30 min)
   - Show pilot GPs how to access templates
   - Walk through completing assessment
   - Demonstrate PHQ-9 auto-scoring
   - Show how to generate patient summary

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Templates downloaded and imported into EHR
- [ ] Local customizations completed
- [ ] Templates tested with dummy patient
- [ ] Pilot GPs trained on how to use templates
- [ ] Quick reference guide created (1-page: how to access templates)

**Troubleshooting:** If EHR integration challenging, have paper versions ready as backup for Week 2 pilot

## Day 4 (Thursday): Staff Training + Patient Materials

**Half-Day Workshop for All Staff** (Receptionists, admin, nurses, HCAs)

**Agenda:**

**9:00-9:30am: Introduction to EQUILIBRIUM**

- How it benefits patients and practice

**9:30-10:00am: Staff Roles in EQUILIBRIUM**
- Reception: Booking longer appointments for new depression assessments
- Admin: Providing patient materials, signposting resources

**10:00-10:30am: PHQ-9 Administration** (if staff will administer)
- How to administer PHQ-9
- Scoring and interpretation
- When to flag urgent (Q9 score >0, total >15)

**10:30-11:00am: Break + Q&A**

**11:00-11:30am: Patient Resources and Signposting**
- EQUILIBRIUM patient guides (tour through 5 guides)
- How to signpost to IAPT, social prescribing, online resources
- Handling common patient questions

**11:30-12:00pm: Practical Session**
- Role-play: Patient asks about depression treatment options
- Practice using patient materials
- Practice signposting to resources

**12:00-1:00pm: Implementation Logistics**
- Timeline for pilot and rollout
- How to support pilot GPs
- Communication with patients about new approach
- Q&A

**Materials Provided to Staff:**
- Quick reference card: Local IAPT contact, social prescribing link worker, crisis numbers
- PHQ-9 scoring card (laminated)
- Patient materials (know where they're located)

**Checklist:**
- [ ] All reception staff attended (or will catch up recording)
- [ ] All admin staff attended
- [ ] Nursing staff attended (if applicable)
- [ ] Staff understand their roles
- [ ] Quick reference materials distributed

---

### Afternoon (2-5pm): Final Pilot Preparation

**Pilot GPs + Practice Manager Meeting** (1 hour)

**Topics:**
1. **Patient Selection for Pilot Week**
   - Identify 5-10 patients each pilot GP will see using EQUILIBRIUM
   - Book 15-20 min appointments (longer than standard)
   - Patients: New presentations of depression OR follow-ups for existing depression

2. **Week 2 Schedule**
   - Which appointments will use EQUILIBRIUM

   - Plan for troubleshooting

3. **Success Metrics**

**Practice Manager: Set Up Tracking** (2 hours)

**Pilot GPs: Review Materials** (2 hours)
- Re-read biopsychosocial assessment template
- Review patient handouts (will give these to patients)
- Practice shared decision-making phrases
- Mental walk-through of first consultation

**Checklist:**
- [ ] 5-10 patients identified per pilot GP
- [ ] Appointments booked (15-20 min slots)
- [ ] Tracking system set up
- [ ] Patient feedback method ready
- [ ] Daily debrief slots booked in GPs' calendars

# Week 2: Pilot Launch

## Day 6 (Monday): Pilot Day 1

### Morning: First EQUILIBRIUM Consultations

**Pilot GPs: See 2-3 Patients Each Using EQUILIBRIUM**

**What to do:**
1. Use biopsychosocial assessment template
2. Discuss lifestyle interventions as treatment options
3. Use shared decision-making
4. Give patient handouts
5. Agree on treatment plan
6. Complete PHQ-9
7. Book follow-up

**What to track:**
- Time taken for consultation
- PHQ-9 score
- Treatment recommended
- How patient responded
- Challenges encountered

**Expect:**
- First few consultations will feel awkward and slow
- You'll forget parts of the assessment
- Patients may seem surprised by comprehensive questions
- You'll refer to templates/notes frequently
- **This is normal and expected!**

---

### Midday: Quick Check-In

**15-Min Pilot GP + Practice Manager Meeting** (12:30-12:45pm)

**Questions:**
- How did first consultations go?
- Any immediate issues?
- Do we need to adjust anything this afternoon?
- How are you feeling?

**Troubleshooting on the spot**

---

### Afternoon: Continue Pilot

**See 1-2 More Patients**

Should feel slightly easier than morning consultations.

---

### End of Day: Debrief

**30-Min Pilot Team Debrief** (5-5:30pm)

**Discuss:**
1. What went well?
2. What was challenging?
3. How did patients respond?
4. Any "a-ha" moments?
5. Adjustments for tomorrow?

**Practice Manager:** Record key learnings in pilot log

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Pilot GPs saw 3-5 patients each using EQUILIBRIUM
- [ ] All consultations tracked
- [ ] Debrief completed, learnings recorded
- [ ] Adjustments identified for Day 2

---

## Day 7-9 (Tuesday-Thursday): Continue Pilot

**Each Day:**

**Morning:**
- Pilot GPs see 2-4 patients using EQUILIBRIUM
- Should get faster and more comfortable each day

**Midday:**
- Quick 10-min check-in
- Address any issues

**Afternoon:**
- Continue seeing patients
- By Day 3, aim for consultations taking 12-15 min (closer to normal)

**End of Day:**
- 20-min debrief
- Share successes
- Problem-solve challenges
- Update tracking data

**What You Should Notice by Thursday:**
- GPs getting more fluent with biopsychosocial assessment
- Consultations taking less time
- Some patients expressing positive feedback
- GPs feeling more confident
- Identifying patterns (e.g., most patients have sleep issues)

## Day 10 (Friday): Midpoint Evaluation

### Morning: Final Pilot Consultations

**Pilot GPs See Last Pilot Patients**

By now, consultations should feel relatively smooth.

---

### Afternoon: Comprehensive Pilot Evaluation

**2-Hour Pilot Team Meeting** (2-4pm)

**Participants:** Pilot GPs, practice manager, any partners interested

**Agenda:**

**1. Review Data** (30 min)
- How many patients seen using EQUILIBRIUM? (Target: 8-15 per GP)
- PHQ-9 scores (baseline data)
- What treatments offered? (medication %, lifestyle %, therapy %, combination %)
- Patient feedback summary
- Time per consultation (trend over week)

**2. GP Reflections** (30 min)
- What worked well?
- What was challenging?
- How do you feel about EQUILIBRIUM approach?
- Would you continue using it?
- What would make it easier?

**3. Patient Feedback** (15 min)
- What did patients say?
- Any common themes?
- Positive responses?
- Concerns?

**4. Adjustments Needed** (30 min)
- EHR templates: Any changes needed?
- Patient materials: Which handouts most useful?
- Training: Any gaps?
- Workflow: Any tweaks to make it smoother?

**5. Decision: Continue? Expand?** (15 min)
- Do pilot GPs want to continue using EQUILIBRIUM? (Should be YES if pilot went reasonably well)
- Are we ready to train more GPs?
- Timeline for expansion?

**Likely Decision:** Continue pilot for 2 more weeks, then train next wave of 2-3 GPs

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Data reviewed and summarized
- [ ] GP and patient feedback collected
- [ ] Adjustments identified and plan to implement
- [ ] Decision made on next steps
- [ ] Timeline for expansion agreed

# Weeks 3-4: Refinement and Preparation for Expansion

## Week 3: Refine and Adjust

**Pilot GPs:**
- Continue using EQUILIBRIUM with all appropriate patients
- Implement adjustments identified in midpoint evaluation
- Consultations should now feel routine

**Practice Manager:**
- Update EHR templates based on feedback
- Refine patient materials
- Prepare for training next wave of GPs

**Partners:**
- Review pilot data
- Approve expansion plan and resources

**Metrics to Track:**
- PHQ-9 scores at follow-up (starting to see treatment response)
- Patient satisfaction scores
- GP satisfaction and confidence
- Time per consultation (should stabilize at 12-15 min)

---

## Week 4: Prepare for Expansion

**Train Next Wave of GPs** (2-3 more GPs)

**Options:**
1. **Send to 2-day intensive training** (if available)
2. **Internal training by pilot GPs** (2-3 hours):
   - Pilot GPs share their experience
   - Demonstrate using EHR templates
   - Walk through case examples
   - Q&A
3. **Online modules** plus mentoring by pilot GPs

**New GPs Start Seeing Patients Using EQUILIBRIUM**

**Pilot GPs Serve as Mentors**
- Available for questions
- Observe new GPs' consultations (if possible)
- Share tips and tricks

**By End of Week 4:**
- 4-6 GPs now using EQUILIBRIUM
- Routine practice for pilot GPs
- New GPs building confidence

# Month 3: Evaluation and Sustainability

## 3-Month Audit

**Data to Collect:**
- Total patients assessed using EQUILIBRIUM biopsychosocial approach
- PHQ-9 response rates (≥50% reduction at follow-up)
- Treatment patterns:
  - % mild depression prescribed medication first-line (target: <20%)
  - % offered lifestyle interventions
  - % referred to therapy
- Patient satisfaction scores
- GP satisfaction and confidence scores

**Compare to Baseline:**
- If practice did pre-implementation audit, compare

**Likely Outcomes After 3 Months:**
- More patients receiving holistic assessment
- Fewer inappropriate antidepressant prescriptions
- More lifestyle interventions and therapy referrals
- Improved patient satisfaction
- GP satisfaction with having "more tools in toolbox"

---

## Sustainability Planning

**Meeting: Partners + All GPs + Practice Manager** (1 hour)

**Topics:**
1. **Celebrate Successes**
   - Share positive outcomes data
   - Share patient testimonials
   - Recognize GP efforts

2. **Identify Challenges**
   - What's still difficult?
   - Any GPs struggling?
   - Workflow issues?

3. **Plan for Sustainment**
   - How do we maintain EQUILIBRIUM long-term?
   - Onboarding new GPs who join practice?
   - Ongoing training and support?
   - Quarterly audits?

4. **Continuous Improvement**
   - Based on 3 months experience, what should we change?
   - Update templates?
   - Different patient materials?
   - More training needed in specific areas?

**Decisions:**
- Continue EQUILIBRIUM indefinitely (should be YES)
- Quarterly audit and review meetings
- Annual refresher training for all GPs
- New GP onboarding includes EQUILIBRIUM training

# Quick-Start Success Factors

## What Makes Quick-Start Work?

1. **High Readiness** - Practice already had prerequisites in place
2. **Strong Leadership** - Partners fully committed, resources allocated
3. **Enthusiastic Champions** - Pilot GPs genuinely wanted to change practice
4. **Rapid Action** - Momentum maintained, didn't lose energy
5. **Learning Mindset** - Embraced imperfection, iterated quickly
6. **Support Infrastructure** - Practice manager coordinating, troubleshooting in real-time
7. **Celebration** - Recognized wins, maintained positivity

---

## Potential Pitfalls and How to Avoid

### Pitfall 1: Rushing Training

**Risk:** GPs feel underprepared, consultations go poorly, abandon EQUILIBRIUM

**How to Avoid:**
- Don't skip training (tempting when moving fast, but essential)
- Minimum 2-day intensive OR complete online modules thoroughly
- Practice with templates before seeing patients
- Mentoring/support during pilot week

---

### Pitfall 2: Pilot GPs Overwhelmed

**Risk:** GPs feel like guinea pigs, too much pressure, burnout

**How to Avoid:**
- Start with small numbers (2-3 patients/day in pilot week)
- Daily debriefs for support
- Normalize the learning curve ("First week will be awkward")
- Celebrate small wins
- Don't demand perfection

---

### Pitfall 3: Losing Momentum

**Risk:** Good pilot week, then everyone gets busy and EQUILIBRIUM fades

**How to Avoid:**
- Schedule expansion timeline upfront (don't leave it vague)
- Practice manager maintains momentum
- Regular check-ins and accountability
- Track data to show progress
- Celebrate milestones

---

### Pitfall 4: No Time for Reflection

**Risk:** Move too fast, don't learn from pilot, repeat mistakes

**How to Avoid:**
- Mandatory midpoint evaluation (Day 10)
- Daily debriefs (even if brief)
- Document learnings in real-time
- Adjust templates/workflow based on feedback
- Balance speed with reflection

**Resources:**

- EQUILIBRIUM training materials and online support
- PCN colleagues who've implemented

**Common Questions:**

**Q: What if pilot goes poorly?**
A: Pause, regroup. Identify what went wrong. More training? Workflow issues? Adjust and try again. Even 1-2 week delay isn't failure.

**Q: What if only 1 GP wants to continue after pilot?**
A: That's OK! One GP can continue while others observe. Success will recruit others over time.

**Q: What if we realize we need more time?**
A: Switch to standard 3-6 month timeline. Quick-start isn't for everyone. Better to implement well over 6 months than poorly in 2 weeks.
