# GP Practice Pilot Application

## About the Pilot Program

Project EQUILIBRIUM is seeking 20 GP practices to pilot holistic mental health clinical pathways (2026-2028).

## Why Become a Pilot Practice?

### Benefits

**For Your Practice:**
- Evidence-based clinical pathway toolkit
- Comprehensive practitioner training (3 days, CPD accredited)
- Enhanced payment for holistic mental health consultations
- Ongoing clinical supervision and support
- Peer practice network
- Contribution to healthcare transformation

**For Your GPs:**
- Wider therapeutic toolbox beyond medication
- Professional development and new skills
- Increased professional satisfaction
- CPD credits (18 credits for initial training)
- Access to latest evidence and best practices

**For Your Patients:**
- Full range of evidence-based treatment options
- Shared decision-making
- Holistic assessment addressing root causes
- Better long-term outcomes
- Choice in treatment approach

### Support Provided

- **3-day training workshop**: All core content and skills
- **Clinical pathways**: Depression, anxiety, mixed presentations
- **EHR templates**: Documentation and decision support
- **Patient materials**: Information handouts and decision aids
- **Clinical supervision**: First 10 cases supervised
- **Ongoing support**: Regular calls, peer network, consultation
- **Resource directory**: Local services and referral pathways
- **Financial support**: Training costs covered, implementation support

## Selection Criteria

We will select 20 practices based on:

### Essential Criteria

✓ **Commitment**: At least 2 GPs committed to participation
✓ **Partnership support**: Practice manager and partners agree
✓ **Capacity**: Time for training and longer initial appointments
✓ **Timeline**: Able to start February 2026
✓ **Data collection**: Willingness to collect outcome data
✓ **Duration**: Minimum 18-month commitment

### Desirable Criteria

- Practice size diversity (small, medium, large)
- Geographic diversity across UK
- Serving diverse populations
- Interest in innovation
- Previous quality improvement experience
- Mental health special interest
- Teaching practice status

- Indicates interest and provides basic information

### Required Materials

**Expression of Interest:**

- Key contacts
- Brief description of current mental health care

**Full Application (if invited):**

   - Practice demographics and patient population

   - Practice manager (required)
   - Lead GP (required)

3. **Implementation Plan**
   - Which GPs will participate
   - Training attendance plan
   - Appointment scheduling approach
   - EHR integration plan
   - Patient communication strategy
   - Local resource mapping
   - Timeline and milestones

4. **Evaluation Agreement**
   - Data collection commitment
   - Outcome measurement approach
   - Patient consent process
   - Reporting commitment

5. **Baseline Data**
   - Current prescribing rates
   - Mental health consultation volume
   - IAPT referral rates
   - Any existing mental health quality metrics

## Selection Process

### Evaluation

Applications evaluated on:
- **Readiness**: Capacity and commitment (30%)
- **Quality of plan**: Feasibility and detail (25%)
- **Practice fit**: Population served and practice characteristics (20%)
- **Diversity**: Geographic and demographic diversity of cohort (15%)
- **Learning potential**: Likelihood of generating insights (10%)

### Decision

- **October 2025**: Selection committee review
- **Mid-October**: Practice visits or video calls (finalists)
- **Late October**: Final decisions
- **Early November**: Notification and agreements

### Not Selected?

If not selected for pilot:
- Feedback provided
- Join scale-up phase (2027+)
- Access to pathway materials when available
- Invitation to learning community

## What to Expect as a Pilot Practice

### Preparation Phase (Nov 2025 - Jan 2026)

- Partnership agreement finalized
- Baseline data collection
- Systems preparation (EHR, appointments, etc.)
- Resource directory development
- Patient information materials prepared
- Staff briefing

### Training (January 2026)

**3-Day Intensive Workshop:**

**Day 1**: Foundations
- EQUILIBRIUM overview and evidence
- Biopsychosocial assessment
- Clinical pathways introduction
- Shared decision-making

**Day 2**: Interventions
- Behavioral activation and CBT basics
- Motivational interviewing
- Lifestyle interventions (exercise, nutrition, sleep)
- Social prescribing
- Mindfulness and stress management

**Day 3**: Implementation
- Pathway navigation and case studies
- EHR templates and documentation
- Role plays and skills practice
- Safety and risk management
- Practice setup and launch planning

**Plus**: 18 CPD credits, comprehensive resource pack

### Implementation Phase (Feb 2026 - Aug 2027)

**Months 1-3 (Launch):**
- Soft launch with selected patients
- Weekly support calls
- Clinical supervision (first 10 cases per GP)
- Rapid problem-solving
- Peer practice connections

**Months 4-12:**
- Full implementation
- Monthly support calls
- Ongoing peer learning (monthly meetings)
- Quarterly data review
- Pathway refinement based on experience

**Months 13-18:**
- Consolidated practice
- Advanced topics webinars
- Outcome data analysis
- Dissemination activities
- Sustainability planning

### Time Commitment

**Per Practice:**
- Implementation planning: 10-15 hours (preparation)
- Data collection/reporting: 2-3 hours/month
- Coordination: 2-3 hours/month

**Per GP:**
- Training: 3 days initial
- Supervision: 5-10 hours (first 3 months)
- Peer learning: 1 hour/month (optional)
- Clinical time: Extended initial appointments (20-30 min vs. 10 min), compensated by enhanced payment

## Enhanced Payment Model

*Where commissioned by local CCG/ICS*

**Holistic Mental Health Consultation:**
- Initial assessment: £80-100 (for 20-30 min appointment)
- Follow-up: Standard rates
- Quality bonus: Based on outcomes and appropriate prescribing
- Support services: Funding for social prescribing link worker (where applicable)

**Business Case:**
- Enhanced payment covers extended time
- Reduced follow-up needs and long-term workload
- Better outcomes reduce overall healthcare costs
- Quality bonuses reward good practice
