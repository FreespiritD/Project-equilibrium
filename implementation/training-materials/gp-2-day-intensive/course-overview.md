# EQUILIBRIUM 2-Day GP Intensive Training Course

**Comprehensive training in evidence-based, holistic approaches to depression management**

---

## Course Overview

This intensive 2-day course equips GPs with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to implement EQUILIBRIUM principles in their clinical practice. Participants learn to:

1. Conduct comprehensive biopsychosocial assessments
2. Offer evidence-based lifestyle interventions as first-line treatment
3. Engage patients in shared decision-making
4. Monitor outcomes systematically
5. Safely deprescribe antidepressants when appropriate

**Format:** Interactive workshop with lectures, case discussions, role-plays, and practical skills sessions

**Duration:** 2 full days (9am-5pm each day, 14 contact hours)

**Group size:** 12-20 GPs optimal, 30 maximum

**CPD Credits:** 14 hours CPD accredited by RCGP

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, participants will be able to:

### Day 1: Evidence and Assessment
- Explain the evidence base for lifestyle interventions in depression (effect sizes, NNT, comparative efficacy)
- Describe the limitations of antidepressants for mild-moderate depression
- Interpret NICE guidelines CG90 for stepped care
- Conduct a structured biopsychosocial assessment using EQUILIBRIUM framework
- Identify biological, psychological, and social contributors to depression
- Administer and interpret PHQ-9

### Day 2: Treatment and Implementation
- Engage patients in shared decision-making using the 3-talk model
- Prescribe evidence-based lifestyle interventions (exercise, nutrition, sleep, social connection)
- Develop personalized treatment plans integrating multiple modalities
- Monitor outcomes using measurement-based care
- Safely deprescribe antidepressants with patient-centered tapering
- Troubleshoot common implementation barriers

## Learning Outcomes Assessment

### Knowledge Assessment
**Pre-post test (10 multiple-choice questions):**
- Evidence for lifestyle interventions (3 questions)
- NICE guidelines interpretation (2 questions)
- Biopsychosocial assessment (2 questions)
- Shared decision-making (2 questions)
- Deprescribing (1 question)

**Target:** ≥80% of participants achieve >70% on post-test

### Skills Assessment (During Course)
**Role-play observation checklist:**
- Conducted comprehensive biopsychosocial assessment
- Explored psychological and social factors (not just biological)
- Used open-ended questions
- Demonstrated empathy
- Proposed treatment plan incorporating lifestyle interventions
- Engaged patient in shared decision-making

**Target:** ≥90% of participants demonstrate competence in observed role-plays

### Confidence Assessment
**Pre-post confidence ratings (1-10 scale):**
- "How confident are you in conducting a biopsychosocial assessment?"
- "How confident are you in prescribing exercise as a treatment for depression?"
- "How confident are you in engaging patients in shared decision-making?"
- "How confident are you in safely deprescribing antidepressants?"

**Target:** Mean confidence increase ≥2 points across all domains

## Adaptation for Different Contexts

### Virtual Delivery (Online 2-Day Course)
**Platform:** Zoom or Microsoft Teams
**Modifications:**
- Shorten sessions to 45-60 min with frequent breaks (screen fatigue)
- Use breakout rooms for small group work
- Ship participant handbooks in advance (or provide PDFs)
- Shorter total contact time: 10-12 hours over 2 days (vs 14 in-person)
- Lower fee: £195-245

**Pros:** Accessible, lower cost, no travel
**Cons:** Less interactive, harder to build community, fatigue

### 4 Half-Day Format (Evenings or Mornings)
- Week 1 (Tue + Thu evenings): Day 1 content split across 2 × 3-hour sessions
- Week 2 (Tue + Thu evenings): Day 2 content split across 2 × 3-hour sessions
- Better for GPs who can't take 2 full days off
- Allows time to practice between sessions

### 1-Day Abbreviated Course
**For contexts with time/budget constraints:**
- Condense to 9am-5pm single day (7 contact hours)
- Focus on essentials: Evidence base, biopsychosocial assessment, lifestyle interventions, SDM
- Skip or shorten: Deprescribing, advanced monitoring
- Lower fee: £195-245
- **Trade-off:** Less depth, less practice time
