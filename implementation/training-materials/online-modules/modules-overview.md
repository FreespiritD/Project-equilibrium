# EQUILIBRIUM Online Learning Modules

**Self-paced e-learning for GPs and practice staff**

---

## Overview

The EQUILIBRIUM online learning modules provide flexible, self-paced training for GPs, nurses, and practice staff who cannot attend in-person workshops or prefer online learning. The modules are designed for independent study, with each module taking approximately 30 minutes to complete.

**Total learning time:** 3-4 hours (6 modules)

**Format:** Online, self-paced

**Platform:** Compatible with standard Learning Management Systems (LMS) or standalone web access

**CPD Accreditation:** 3-4 hours CPD-accredited by RCGP

**Cost:** Free (for practices implementing EQUILIBRIUM)

---

## Target Audience

**Primary:**
- GPs unable to attend 2-day intensive course
- GP trainees (ST1-ST3)
- Practice nurses managing depression
- Salaried GPs and locums

**Also suitable for:**
- Medical students on GP placements
- Advanced nurse practitioners
- PCN clinical pharmacists supporting mental health

**Prerequisites:**
- Medical or nursing qualification
- Current clinical practice (or recent within 2 years)
- Basic familiarity with depression management

---

## Module Structure

### 6 Core Modules (30 minutes each)

| Module | Title | Content | Learning Outcomes |
|--------|-------|---------|-------------------|
| **1** | Evidence Base for Holistic Approaches | Lifestyle interventions (exercise, nutrition, sleep), effect sizes, mechanisms | Cite evidence for lifestyle interventions; compare efficacy to antidepressants |
| **2** | NICE Guidelines and Appropriate Prescribing | Stepped care, limitations of antidepressants, guideline-concordant practice | Interpret NICE CG90; explain why medication NOT first-line for mild depression |
| **3** | Biopsychosocial Assessment | Assessment framework, questioning skills, case examples | Conduct structured biopsychosocial assessment; identify modifiable contributors |
| **4** | Prescribing Lifestyle Interventions | Exercise prescription, nutrition guidance, sleep interventions, practical tools | Prescribe specific lifestyle interventions; use templates and patient resources |
| **5** | Shared Decision-Making | 3-talk model, presenting options, eliciting preferences | Apply SDM using 3-talk model; support patient decision-making |
| **6** | Monitoring and Deprescribing | Measurement-based care (PHQ-9), interpreting changes, safe tapering | Implement PHQ-9 monitoring; develop safe deprescribing plans |

## Module Descriptions

### Module 1: Evidence Base for Holistic Approaches
**Duration:** 30 minutes

**Content:**
1. **Introduction (5 min):** The biopsychosocial model of depression
2. **Exercise as treatment (8 min):**
   - Meta-analysis evidence (effect size 0.62)
   - Dose-response (moderate intensity, 150 min/week)
   - Mechanisms (BDNF, inflammation, HPA axis)
   - Comparative efficacy to antidepressants
3. **Nutrition (Mediterranean diet) (7 min):**
   - SMILES trial (32% remission, NNT = 4)
   - Dietary components (increase vegetables, fish, whole grains; reduce processed foods)
   - Gut-brain axis and inflammation
4. **Sleep interventions (CBT-I) (5 min):**
   - Bidirectional relationship (insomnia ↔ depression)
   - CBT-I effect on depression (NNT = 6)
   - Brief interventions GPs can deliver
5. **Social connection and behavioral activation (3 min):**
   - Social isolation as risk factor
   - Social prescribing evidence
   - Behavioral activation principles
6. **Summary and quiz (2 min):**
   - 5 multiple-choice questions

**Learning Activities:**
- Video: Patient testimonial on exercise as treatment (3 min)
- Interactive comparison: Effect sizes of different interventions (drag-and-drop)
- Case vignette: Identify biological, psychological, social contributors

**Assessment:**
- 5 multiple-choice questions (must score ≥4/5 to proceed)

---

### Module 2: NICE Guidelines and Appropriate Prescribing
**Duration:** 30 minutes

**Content:**
1. **Introduction (3 min):** Current prescribing patterns (67% non-guideline-concordant)
2. **Evidence on antidepressant efficacy (10 min):**
   - Meta-analysis data (effect size 0.3-0.5)
   - Response rates (drug 50-60%, placebo 40%)
   - Publication bias (Turner et al. 2008)
   - Clinical significance debate
3. **Side effects and discontinuation (5 min):**
   - Common side effects (sexual dysfunction 40-65%, weight gain, emotional blunting)
   - Discontinuation rates (40-60% within 3 months)
   - Withdrawal symptoms (50% experience)
4. **NICE CG90 Stepped Care Model (8 min):**
   - Step 1-4 framework
   - Key guideline statement: "Do NOT routinely offer antidepressant medication as first-line for mild depression"
   - Rationale (poor risk-benefit ratio)
   - Exceptions (patient preference, previous response, moderate-severe)
5. **Guideline-concordant practice (2 min):**
   - Aligning practice with NICE recommendations
   - When medication is appropriate
6. **Quiz (2 min):**
   - 5 multiple-choice questions

**Learning Activities:**
- Interactive decision tree: Apply stepped care to case scenarios (mild, moderate, severe depression)
- Reflection: "Think of a recent patient with mild depression. Did your management align with NICE guidelines?"

**Assessment:**
- 5 multiple-choice questions (must score ≥4/5 to proceed)

### Module 3: Biopsychosocial Assessment
**Duration:** 30 minutes

**Content:**
1. **Introduction (5 min):**
   - Biomedical vs biopsychosocial model
   - Why biopsychosocial assessment matters (identifies modifiable factors, personalizes treatment)
2. **The EQUILIBRIUM assessment template (10 min):**
   - Biological factors (sleep, exercise, nutrition, physical health, substances)
   - Psychological factors (thoughts, coping, trauma, stressors, activities)
   - Social factors (relationships, work, housing, finances, isolation)
   - Patient's perspective
3. **Effective questioning skills (5 min):**
   - Open-ended questions
   - Examples for each domain
   - Connecting the dots (formulation)
4. **Video demonstration (8 min):**
   - GP conducting biopsychosocial assessment with simulated patient
   - Debrief: What did GP do well? What factors identified?
5. **Practice case (2 min):**
   - Interactive case: Read scenario, identify biological/psychological/social contributors

**Learning Activities:**
- Video demonstration with annotations (highlighting key questions and techniques)
- Interactive case: Click on biological/psychological/social factors in patient narrative
- Downloadable assessment template

**Assessment:**
- Case-based question: Given patient scenario, identify key contributors and formulate treatment targets

---

### Module 4: Prescribing Lifestyle Interventions
**Duration:** 30 minutes

**Content:**
1. **Introduction (3 min):** Lifestyle interventions as evidence-based treatment (not vague advice)
2. **Exercise prescription (10 min):**
   - Specific dose (type, intensity, duration, frequency)
   - Exercise prescription template
   - Progression (start small, build up)
   - Addressing barriers ("I'm too tired," "I don't have time," "I hate exercise")
   - Resources (NHS Couch to 5K, Active 10, local walking groups)
3. **Nutrition guidance (Mediterranean diet) (8 min):**
   - Practical recommendations (increase vegetables/fish/whole grains, reduce processed foods)
   - Simple swaps (white bread → whole grain, crisps → nuts)
   - Patient handout review
   - Dietitian referral (when appropriate)
4. **Sleep interventions (7 min):**
   - Sleep hygiene education (regular schedule, bedroom environment, screen time, caffeine/alcohol)
   - Brief CBT-I components (stimulus control, sleep restriction overview)
   - Online resources (Sleepio, NHS apps)
5. **Social connection and behavioral activation (2 min):**
   - Social prescribing (link workers, community resources)
   - Behavioral activation (systematic increase in rewarding activities)

**Learning Activities:**
- Interactive: Create exercise prescription for case patient (fill in template)
- Video: GP demonstrating how to "prescribe" exercise with conviction
- Downloadable patient handouts (exercise, nutrition, sleep)

**Assessment:**
- Case scenario: Patient with moderate depression, poor sleep, no exercise, social isolation. Develop lifestyle intervention plan (short answer, review by facilitator or auto-marked rubric)

---

### Module 5: Shared Decision-Making
**Duration:** 30 minutes

**Content:**
1. **Introduction (5 min):**
   - What is SDM? (partnership between clinician and patient)
   - Why it matters (adherence, satisfaction, patient autonomy)
   - Evidence for SDM in depression
2. **The 3-Talk Model (15 min):**
   - **Choice Talk (4 min):** Introduce options and decision
     - Key phrases: "There are several approaches..." "I want to hear what's important to you"
     - Example script
   - **Option Talk (6 min):** Describe options in detail
     - Structure: What it is, effectiveness, what's involved, benefits, downsides
     - Example: Presenting lifestyle, therapy, and medication options
     - Neutrality (avoid bias)
   - **Decision Talk (5 min):** Support patient to express preferences
     - Eliciting values: "What's most important to you?"
     - Giving recommendations when asked: "Based on what you've told me..."
     - Validating decision
3. **Video demonstration (8 min):**
   - GP using 3-talk model with patient
   - Patient chooses lifestyle interventions first, with plan to reassess
4. **Practice scenario (2 min):**
   - Interactive: Choose best SDM responses in conversation with patient

**Learning Activities:**
- Video demonstration with interactive pauses: "What would you say next?" (multiple choice)
- Role-play audio examples: Effective vs ineffective SDM (compare approaches)
- Downloadable SDM conversation guide

**Assessment:**
- Multiple-choice questions (5): Identify best SDM responses in scenarios

---

### Module 6: Monitoring and Deprescribing
**Duration:** 30 minutes

**Content:**
1. **Introduction to measurement-based care (5 min):**
   - What is MBC? (systematic use of PHQ-9 to track progress)
   - Evidence for MBC (improves outcomes, faster remission)
2. **PHQ-9 monitoring (10 min):**
   - Administering and scoring PHQ-9
   - Interpreting changes (response ≥50% reduction, remission <5, non-response <25-30% by 8-12 weeks)
   - Monitoring schedule (baseline, weeks 2, 4, 8, 12)
   - Using PHQ-9 to guide decisions (continue, intensify, escalate)
   - Case example: PHQ-9 trajectory, treatment decisions at each point
3. **Deprescribing antidepressants safely (13 min):**
   - Who is appropriate for deprescribing? (stable >6 months, motivated, circumstances stable)
   - Who should continue? (multiple relapses, severe history, residual symptoms)
   - Tapering principles (slow, hyperbolic taper, monitor closely, patient-led pace)
   - Example tapering schedules (sertraline, citalopram, fluoxetine)
   - Managing withdrawal symptoms
   - Distinguishing withdrawal from relapse
   - Supporting lifestyle interventions during deprescribing (relapse prevention)
4. **Quiz (2 min):** 5 multiple-choice questions

**Learning Activities:**
- Interactive PHQ-9 scoring practice (complete examples)
- Decision tree: Given PHQ-9 trajectory, choose appropriate action (continue, intensify, escalate)
- Tapering schedule calculator: Input medication and dose, generate tapering plan

**Assessment:**
- Case scenario: Patient on sertraline 100mg for 2 years, now in remission, wants to stop. Develop safe deprescribing plan (multiple choice or short answer)

## Learning Pathway and Progression

### Self-Paced Learning
**Recommended completion:**
- **Ideal:** Complete all 6 modules over 1-2 weeks (e.g., 2 modules per week)
- **Minimum:** Complete at own pace over 4 weeks
- **Flexible:** Modules can be completed in order or individually (though sequential recommended)

**Progress tracking:**
- LMS tracks module completion
- Certificate issued after completing all 6 modules and passing assessments

### Blended Learning (Combination)
**Option 1: Online + 1-Day Workshop**
- Complete Modules 1-3 online (self-paced, 1.5 hours)
- Attend 1-day in-person workshop (Modules 4-6 with hands-on practice)
- **Benefit:** Reduces in-person time, preserves practical skills practice

**Option 2: Online + Peer Discussion**
- Complete all 6 modules online individually
- Join 2-hour online facilitated discussion (Q&A, case discussions, problem-solving)
- **Benefit:** Flexibility of online + peer learning

## Assessment and Certification

### Module-Level Assessment
**Each module includes:**
- **Formative assessment:** Interactive activities during module (not graded, immediate feedback)
- **Summative assessment:** 5 multiple-choice or case-based questions at end
- **Pass mark:** ≥80% (4/5 correct)
- **Retake:** Unlimited retakes if <80% (review content and try again)

### Course-Level Assessment
**Final assessment (after completing all 6 modules):**
- **20 questions** (mix of multiple-choice and case-based)
- **Content:** Drawn from all 6 modules
- **Pass mark:** ≥70% (14/20 correct)
- **Time:** 30 minutes
- **Retake:** 2 attempts allowed

### Certificate of Completion
**Upon successful completion:**
- Digital certificate (PDF, downloadable)
- **CPD credits:** 3-4 hours (RCGP-accredited)
- **Certificate details:** Name, date, modules completed, CPD hours

## Comparison: Online Modules vs 2-Day Intensive Course

| Feature | Online Modules | 2-Day Intensive |
|---------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Duration** | 3-4 hours (self-paced) | 14 hours (2 days) |
| **Format** | Self-paced, online | In-person, interactive |
| **Cost** | Free (implementing practices) or £95 (individual) | £395 per GP (£295 trainees) |
| **CPD** | 3-4 hours | 14 hours |
| **Practical skills practice** | Limited (case-based exercises) | Extensive (role-plays, small groups) |
| **Peer learning** | Minimal | Strong (cohort learning) |
| **Flexibility** | High (complete anytime, anywhere) | Low (specific dates, travel required) |
| **Best for** | Time-constrained GPs, remote learners, self-directed learners | GPs preferring interactive learning, hands-on practice, networking |

**Recommendation:**
- **Online modules:** Good foundation, convenient, cost-effective
- **2-day intensive:** More comprehensive, better for skill-building and confidence
- **Blended:** Ideal compromise (online theory + brief in-person practice)

## Evaluation and Continuous Improvement

### Learner Evaluation
**Post-module surveys:**
- "How useful was this module?" (1-5 scale)
- "What was most valuable?"
- "What could be improved?"

**Post-course survey:**
- Knowledge gain: Pre-post quiz comparison (same questions at start and end of course)
- Confidence gain: "How confident are you now in implementing EQUILIBRIUM?" (1-10 scale)
- Implementation intent: "I plan to use EQUILIBRIUM in my practice within the next month" (agree/disagree)

### 3-Month Follow-Up
**Email survey to learners:**
- "Have you implemented EQUILIBRIUM in your practice?" (yes/no)
- "What barriers have you encountered?"
- "What additional support would be helpful?"
- Implementation outcomes: "Approximately how many patients have you managed using EQUILIBRIUM approach?"

### Continuous Improvement
- Review evaluation data quarterly
- Identify low-rated modules or unclear content
- Revise modules based on feedback
- Update evidence base annually (new research on lifestyle interventions, guidelines)
