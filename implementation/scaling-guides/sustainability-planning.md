# Sustainability Planning: Maintaining EQUILIBRIUM Long-Term

**Strategies to embed EQUILIBRIUM in routine practice and prevent implementation decay**

---

## Overview

Implementing EQUILIBRIUM is one challenge; sustaining it long-term is another. Research shows that 60-80% of evidence-based innovations in healthcare fade within 2-3 years due to "implementation decay" - gradual drift back to old practices as initial enthusiasm wanes, champions leave, or competing priorities emerge.

This guide provides evidence-based strategies to sustain EQUILIBRIUM implementation over 5+ years, ensuring that improved depression care becomes "business as usual" rather than a temporary project.

**Target audience:**
- Practice managers and GP partners (practice-level sustainability)
- PCN clinical directors (network-level sustainability)
- ICB mental health leads (system-level sustainability)
- Implementation teams (regional coordination)

---

## Why Implementations Fail to Sustain

### Common Causes of Implementation Decay

**1. Champion Dependency**
- **Problem:** Implementation depends on 1-2 passionate individuals; when they leave, retire, or burn out, EQUILIBRIUM fades
- **Example:** Dr. Smith drives EQUILIBRIUM at practice; she leaves for new job → new GP partners not trained, practice reverts to old prescribing patterns

**2. Competing Priorities**
- **Problem:** New initiatives emerge (QOF changes, PCN DES priorities); EQUILIBRIUM gets crowded out
- **Example:** PCN launches diabetes prevention program, asthma case finding → GPs overwhelmed, EQUILIBRIUM monitoring slips

**3. Staff Turnover**
- **Problem:** GPs, practice managers, nurses leave; replacements not trained in EQUILIBRIUM
- **Example:** 2 of 5 GPs turn over in 2 years → 40% of clinical team doesn't know EQUILIBRIUM approach

**4. Implementation Drift**
- **Problem:** Small deviations accumulate over time (PHQ-9 not always administered, biopsychosocial assessment becomes cursory)
- **Example:** Initially 90% patients received PHQ-9 monitoring; after 2 years, it's 55%

**5. Loss of External Support**
- **Problem:** Implementation team support ends (coaching calls, learning collaboratives discontinued) → practices lose accountability and troubleshooting support
- **Example:** After project funding ends, quarterly learning collaboratives stop; practices feel isolated, engagement drops

**6. Lack of System Integration**
- **Problem:** EQUILIBRIUM remains separate "project" rather than embedded in EHR, protocols, incentives
- **Example:** PHQ-9 monitoring not in EHR template → GPs forget; lifestyle interventions not in formulary/protocol → new GPs unaware

**7. Fatigue and Burnout**
- **Problem:** GPs overwhelmed by workload, burnout → revert to quickest approach (prescribing antidepressants)
- **Example:** Practice under pressure (GP shortage, high patient demand) → GPs cut corners, biopsychosocial assessments become brief, lifestyle prescribing declines

## Sustainability Framework

### Three Levels of Sustainability

**1. Practice-Level Sustainability:**
- EQUILIBRIUM embedded in practice operations, culture, workflows
- Resilient to staff turnover and external pressures

**2. System-Level Sustainability:**
- EQUILIBRIUM integrated into regional infrastructure (training, QI metrics, data systems)
- Supported by PCN/ICB policies and resources

**3. Individual Clinician Sustainability:**
- GPs find EQUILIBRIUM rewarding and manageable
- Prevents burnout and maintains motivation

**All three levels required for long-term sustainability.**

## Practice-Level Sustainability Strategies

### 1. Institutionalize EQUILIBRIUM in Practice Operations

**Goal:** EQUILIBRIUM becomes "the way we do depression" (not optional project)

**Strategies:**

**A. Embed in Practice Protocols and Guidelines**
- **Create practice-level depression protocol:**
  - "All patients presenting with depression receive PHQ-9"
  - "GPs conduct biopsychosocial assessment using template"
  - "Mild-moderate depression: Offer lifestyle interventions as first-line (per NICE CG90)"
  - "PHQ-9 monitoring at weeks 2, 4, 8, 12"
- **Include in practice handbook** (so all staff aware)
- **Review annually** at practice meeting (keeps EQUILIBRIUM visible)

**B. Integrate into EHR Templates**
- **Depression consultation template includes:**
  - PHQ-9 score field (auto-calculates)
  - Biopsychosocial assessment prompts (sleep, exercise, nutrition, social factors)
  - Treatment plan options (lifestyle interventions pre-populated)
  - Follow-up schedule auto-generates (2-week, 4-week appointments)
- **Benefit:** EHR structure prompts GPs to follow EQUILIBRIUM approach (even if distracted or busy)
- **IT lead responsibility:** Update templates, train new staff on template use

**C. Standard Operating Procedures for Staff**
- **Reception SOP:** "For depression appointments, give patient PHQ-9 to complete before seeing GP"
- **Nursing SOP:** "When patient books depression follow-up, check if PHQ-9 needed"
- **Document SOPs** in practice manual (ensures consistency with staff turnover)

---

### 2. Distribute Responsibility (Reduce Champion Dependency)

**Goal:** EQUILIBRIUM not dependent on 1-2 individuals

**Strategies:**

**A. EQUILIBRIUM Practice Team (Not Solo Champion)**
- **Team includes:**
  - **GP lead** (1 partner): Champions EQUILIBRIUM, represents practice at regional learning collaborative
  - **Practice manager:** Ensures workflows, resources, staff training maintained
  - **Nurse lead:** Supports PHQ-9 administration, patient signposting
  - **Administrator:** Manages appointment scheduling, resource stock
- **Team meets quarterly** (15 min) to review EQUILIBRIUM implementation (what's working, what needs attention)
- **Benefit:** If one person leaves, others maintain continuity

**B. All GPs Trained**
- **Ensure 100% of GPs trained in EQUILIBRIUM** (not just 1-2 champions)
- **New GP onboarding:** All new GPs (partners, salaried, locums) complete online EQUILIBRIUM training within first month (see training-materials/online-modules/)
- **Annual refresher:** All GPs attend 1-hour refresher webinar annually (maintains skills, updates on evidence)

**C. Train Replacement Champions**
- **Succession planning:** If GP lead plans to leave, they identify and mentor successor 3-6 months in advance
- **GP lead role description:** Documented responsibilities (so successor knows what to do)

---

### 3. Maintain Resources and Infrastructure

**Goal:** Patient materials, EHR templates, workflows remain functional over time

**Strategies:**

**A. Resource Stewardship**
- **Designate staff member** (practice manager or administrator) to maintain:
  - Patient resource folders stocked (exercise handouts, nutrition guides, sleep resources)
  - Biopsychosocial assessment templates printed and available
  - PHQ-9 forms stocked
- **Quarterly check:** "Do we have adequate EQUILIBRIUM resources?"

**B. EHR Template Maintenance**
- **Annual review:** IT lead or GP lead reviews EHR templates (still functional? Any updates needed based on new evidence?)
- **When EHR system updates:** Ensure templates still work (sometimes system upgrades break custom templates)

**C. Physical Environment Reminders**
- **Posters in consultation rooms:** Biopsychosocial assessment prompt, lifestyle intervention benefits (keeps EQUILIBRIUM visible to GPs)
- **Patient waiting room materials:** EQUILIBRIUM posters ("Our practice uses holistic approaches to depression"), leaflets

---

### 4. Monitor and Audit (Accountability)

**Goal:** Track implementation fidelity and outcomes; intervene if slipping

**Strategies:**

**A. Quarterly Practice Metrics Review**
- **Key metrics** (from EHR extraction or manual audit):
  - % depression consultations where PHQ-9 administered (target: ≥80%)
  - PHQ-9 response rate at 12 weeks (target: ≥60%)
  - % mild depression cases prescribed antidepressant as first-line (target: <30%, ideally <20%)
- **Review at practice meeting** (quarterly, 10 min agenda item)
  - Celebrate successes ("Our PHQ-9 response rate is 64%, up from 62% last quarter")
  - Identify gaps ("PHQ-9 completion rate dropped to 68% - why? How can we improve?")

**B. Annual Case Audit**
- **Once per year, audit 20-30 depression cases** (using EQUILIBRIUM audit tool)
  - % with biopsychosocial assessment documented
  - % offered lifestyle interventions
  - % with PHQ-9 monitoring completed
- **Report findings** to practice team
- **Action plan:** If fidelity <70%, implement corrective actions (e.g., GP refresher training, workflow review)

**C. Patient Feedback**
- **Annual patient survey** (sample of 30-50 depression patients)
  - "Did your GP explore factors like sleep, exercise, social situation?" (yes/no)
  - "Were you offered options for treatment (medication, lifestyle changes, therapy)?" (yes/no)
  - "How satisfied are you with your depression care?" (1-5 scale)
- **Purpose:** Patient perspective on whether EQUILIBRIUM principles being followed

---

### 5. Celebrate and Recognize

**Goal:** Sustain motivation and engagement

**Strategies:**

**A. Internal Recognition**
- **Quarterly practice meeting:** Acknowledge team for EQUILIBRIUM outcomes ("Thanks to everyone's efforts, our PHQ-9 response rates are excellent")
- **Individual recognition:** Thank specific staff ("Jane, your consistent PHQ-9 administration is a huge part of our success")

**B. External Recognition**
- **Submit for awards:** NHS Quality Improvement awards, RCGP Quality Awards, regional GP excellence awards
- **Showcase at conferences:** GP champion presents practice outcomes at regional or national conferences (builds pride, raises practice profile)

**C. Patient Stories**
- **Share patient testimonials** (with consent) at practice meetings
- **Example:** "Mrs. P wrote a thank-you note: 'For the first time, my doctor addressed why I was depressed, not just gave me pills. I'm so much better now.'"
- **Purpose:** Reminds team of impact (sustains sense of purpose)

## System-Level Sustainability Strategies

### 6. Integrate EQUILIBRIUM into Regional Infrastructure

**Goal:** EQUILIBRIUM supported by PCN/ICB systems (not dependent on time-limited project funding)

**Strategies:**

**A. Embed in PCN Directed Enhanced Service (DES)**
- **Proposal to ICB:** Include EQUILIBRIUM-aligned metrics in PCN mental health DES
  - E.g., "% depression patients with PHQ-9 documented at baseline and follow-up" (already QOF-adjacent)
  - "% mild depression cases with documented offer of non-pharmacological intervention"
- **Benefit:** Financial incentive sustains practice attention to EQUILIBRIUM

**B. Include in ICB Quality Improvement (QI) Framework**
- **ICB mental health transformation plan:** Explicitly name EQUILIBRIUM as regional strategy for depression care
- **PCN QI priorities:** EQUILIBRIUM listed as core PCN mental health initiative (signals importance)

**C. Regional Training Infrastructure**
- **Permanent EQUILIBRIUM training program:**
  - Quarterly 2-day GP intensive courses (not one-off project deliveries)
  - Online modules available perpetually (not time-limited project website)
  - Practice staff workshops offered annually to all PCN practices
- **Funding:** ICB education budget or PCN DES education allocation (not grant-dependent)

**D. Data Systems Integration**
- **Automated reporting:**
  - ICB/PCN dashboards include EQUILIBRIUM metrics (PHQ-9 response rates, guideline-concordant prescribing)
  - Practices receive quarterly automated reports (no manual data submission)
- **Benefit:** Reduces burden, maintains visibility

---

### 7. Sustain Learning Collaborative and Peer Networks

**Goal:** Maintain community of practice for ongoing support, learning, troubleshooting

**Strategies:**

**A. Quarterly Learning Collaborative Sessions (Continue Indefinitely)**
- **Format:** 2-hour session, all EQUILIBRIUM practices invited
- **Content:**
  - Outcomes data sharing (anonymized practice comparisons)
  - Case discussions (challenging patients)
  - Innovation sharing ("Practice X found a great workflow for PHQ-9 administration")
  - Guest speakers (lifestyle medicine experts, patients)
- **Funding:** ICB QI budget or small practice contribution (£50/year per practice)

**B. Annual Regional EQUILIBRIUM Conference**
- **Large event (200+ attendees):**
  - Keynote speakers
  - Practice presentations (showcase innovations)
  - Workshops (advanced topics like deprescribing, health equity)
  - Networking
- **Benefit:** Celebrates regional achievement, maintains visibility, sustains community
- **Funding:** Sponsorship (pharmaceutical companies interested in deprescribing, lifestyle medicine organizations), registration fees

**C. Online Community Platform**
- **Discussion forum or WhatsApp group:**
  - GPs ask questions, share experiences asynchronously
  - "How do you all handle patients who refuse to try exercise?" (crowd-sourced advice)
- **Moderated** by implementation lead or volunteer GP champion

---

### 8. Continuous Quality Improvement (CQI) Cycle

**Goal:** EQUILIBRIUM evolves and improves over time (not static)

**Strategies:**

**A. Annual Regional Review**
- **Steering group** (PCN clinical directors, ICB lead, implementation team) meets annually
  - Review regional outcomes (are PHQ-9 response rates sustained? Improving?)
  - Identify challenges (e.g., "Deprescribing rates still low - need more training")
  - Set annual QI goals (e.g., "Year 5 goal: Increase PHQ-9 completion rate to 85%")

**B. PDSA Cycles for Innovation**
- **Test new approaches:**
  - E.g., "Can group exercise prescription programs improve adherence?" (pilot in 3 practices, evaluate, spread if successful)
  - E.g., "Can pharmacist-led deprescribing clinics support GPs?" (test and scale)
- **Engage practices in QI:** Invite practices to propose and test innovations (builds ownership)

**C. Update Based on Emerging Evidence**
- **Annual evidence review:** Clinical lead reviews new research on lifestyle interventions, depression management
- **Update training materials and guidelines** if significant new evidence emerges
  - E.g., "New RCT on yoga for depression - should we add yoga to our exercise resources?"

## Individual Clinician Sustainability Strategies

### 9. Prevent GP Burnout and Maintain Engagement

**Goal:** GPs find EQUILIBRIUM rewarding, manageable, and professionally satisfying

**Strategies:**

**A. Ensure Workload Feasibility**
- **Reality check:** Is EQUILIBRIUM adding excessive time?
  - If yes: Simplify workflows (e.g., nurse administers PHQ-9, GP just reviews score), provide templates to speed documentation
  - Monitor GP feedback: Annual survey - "Is EQUILIBRIUM sustainable in your current workload?" (if no, address)

**B. Emphasize Intrinsic Rewards**
- **Connect to values:** Remind GPs why they became doctors (helping patients, addressing root causes)
- **Patient outcomes:** Share success stories (patients who achieved remission, avoided long-term antidepressants)
- **Professional satisfaction:** Many GPs find EQUILIBRIUM more fulfilling than "prescribe and repeat" model

**C. Peer Support**
- **Learning collaborative** provides community (normalizes challenges, celebrates successes)
- **Buddy system:** Pair newer EQUILIBRIUM GPs with experienced ones (for informal support)

**D. Professional Development**
- **Opportunities for leadership:**
  - Become peer recruiter (see peer-recruitment-strategies.md)
  - Faculty development (train other GPs)
  - Research opportunities (publish practice outcomes, participate in multi-site studies)
- **CPD credits:** Recognize EQUILIBRIUM implementation as CPD-worthy activity (RCGP approves)

---

### 10. Make EQUILIBRIUM Easy (Reduce Friction)

**Goal:** EQUILIBRIUM workflows streamlined so GPs choose them naturally (path of least resistance)

**Strategies:**

**A. EHR Smart Templates**
- **Auto-populate:** Template pre-fills patient name, date, previous PHQ-9 scores (GP just updates)
- **Decision support:** If patient PHQ-9 = 12 (moderate), template suggests "Consider: Exercise prescription, nutrition counseling, online CBT"
- **One-click resources:** Links to patient handouts (GP clicks, prints for patient in seconds)

**B. Pre-Visit Preparation**
- **Reception or nurse pre-work:**
  - PHQ-9 already completed when patient sees GP
  - Previous PHQ-9 scores flagged in chart ("Baseline 14 → 4 weeks ago 10 → today ___")
- **GP has data immediately** (no hunting for old scores)

**C. Patient Handouts Ready**
- **Pre-printed folders at reception:**
  - GP says "Give patient EQUILIBRIUM folder" → reception hands patient comprehensive resource pack
  - GP doesn't spend time explaining resources (handouts do the work)

**D. Batch Efficiency**
- **Booking templates:** Double-slot depression appointments (15-20 min) automatically
- **Follow-up auto-scheduling:** When GP books 2-week follow-up, template auto-prompts "Give PHQ-9"

## Sustainability Indicators and Monitoring

### Key Indicators of Sustainable Implementation

**Green flags (implementation sustained):**
- ✅ PHQ-9 completion rates maintained or improving (≥75%)
- ✅ Outcomes sustained (PHQ-9 response rates ≥55%)
- ✅ Guideline-concordant prescribing maintained (mild depression first-line medication <35%)
- ✅ Staff turnover managed (new staff trained within 1 month)
- ✅ GP satisfaction high (≥70% report EQUILIBRIUM "valuable and feasible")
- ✅ Practice metrics reviewed quarterly (accountability maintained)

**Yellow flags (sustainability at risk - intervene):**
- ⚠️ PHQ-9 completion rates declining (e.g., 85% → 65%)
- ⚠️ Outcomes plateauing or slight decline (response rates 62% → 56%)
- ⚠️ Staff turnover high and training not keeping pace
- ⚠️ GP champion left, no clear successor
- ⚠️ Practice metrics not reviewed for 6+ months (lost accountability)
- ⚠️ GP feedback: "Too busy, EQUILIBRIUM slipping"

**Red flags (implementation decay - urgent intervention):**
- ❌ PHQ-9 completion rates <50% (lost fidelity)
- ❌ Outcomes back to baseline (response rates 45% like pre-EQUILIBRIUM)
- ❌ Prescribing patterns reverted (mild depression medication first-line 60%+)
- ❌ No GP champion, no team ownership
- ❌ Practice disengaged from learning collaborative (not attending)
- ❌ GPs report "We don't really do EQUILIBRIUM anymore"

### Intervention Based on Flags

**If yellow flags detected:**
- **Practice-level intervention:**
  - Implementation lead contacts practice (phone call or visit)
  - Identify barriers ("What's making it hard to sustain EQUILIBRIUM?")
  - Problem-solve (workflow adjustments, refresher training, resource restocking)
  - Set 90-day goal ("Let's get PHQ-9 completion back to 75%") and check in monthly

**If red flags detected:**
- **Intensive intervention:**
  - Practice assessment visit (observe workflows, interview GPs/staff)
  - Identify root causes (champion left? Competing priorities? Workflow broken?)
  - Develop action plan with practice team
  - Booster training (half-day refresher for all GPs)
  - Weekly check-ins for 3 months
- **If unsuccessful:** Document learning ("Why did implementation fail here?"), focus resources on practices more likely to sustain

## Case Example: Sustaining EQUILIBRIUM Over 5 Years

### Hillside Medical Centre (Urban 6-GP Practice)

**Year 0:** Implemented EQUILIBRIUM (pilot phase)

**Year 1:** Excellent outcomes (PHQ-9 response 63%), high fidelity (PHQ-9 completion 92%), GP enthusiasm high

**Year 2:** **Challenge - Champion Left**
- Dr. Roberts (GP champion) took sabbatical
- PHQ-9 completion dropped to 78%
- **Intervention:** Practice manager stepped up as interim lead, ensured new salaried GP completed online EQUILIBRIUM training, Dr. Roberts briefed locum on EQUILIBRIUM protocols
- **Outcome:** Fidelity recovered to 85% by end of Year 2

**Year 3:** **Challenge - Competing Priorities**
- PCN launched diabetes prevention program, COVID-19 booster campaign
- GPs overwhelmed, EQUILIBRIUM monitoring became inconsistent (65% PHQ-9 completion)
- **Intervention:** Practice team quarterly review identified issue; practice manager assigned nurse to administer PHQ-9 (reduced GP burden)
- **Outcome:** PHQ-9 completion improved to 80%

**Year 4:** **Challenge - Staff Turnover**
- 2 GPs left, 2 new GPs hired (40% turnover)
- New GPs unfamiliar with EQUILIBRIUM
- **Intervention:** Practice manager ensured both new GPs completed online training in first month; Dr. Roberts mentored them (shadowed consultations, reviewed cases)
- **Outcome:** New GPs competent in EQUILIBRIUM by 6 months

**Year 5:** **Sustained Success**
- PHQ-9 completion 82% (sustained)
- PHQ-9 response rate 61% (sustained, slightly better than Year 1)
- Guideline-concordant prescribing maintained (22% mild depression medication first-line)
- GP satisfaction high (all GPs report EQUILIBRIUM "integrated into routine practice")

**Key success factors:**
- **Distributed responsibility:** When champion left, practice manager and team maintained continuity
- **EHR integration:** Templates prompted EQUILIBRIUM approach even when GPs busy
- **Problem-solving:** Practice team identified slippage early and intervened
- **Training new staff:** All new GPs trained within first month
- **Regional support:** Learning collaborative provided troubleshooting support during challenges

## Conclusion

Sustainability requires proactive planning, multi-level strategies (practice, system, individual clinician), and ongoing monitoring. EQUILIBRIUM is more likely to sustain when:

1. **Embedded in operations:** Protocols, EHR templates, workflows, SOPs (not dependent on champion memory)
2. **Distributed responsibility:** Team ownership, all GPs trained (not solo champion)
3. **Monitored:** Quarterly metrics, annual audits (accountability)
4. **Supported:** Learning collaborative, peer networks, refresher training (not isolated practices)
5. **Integrated into systems:** PCN DES, ICB priorities, regional training (not time-limited project)
6. **Rewarding for clinicians:** Manageable workload, patient outcomes, professional satisfaction (not burnout-inducing)

**Timeline:** Sustainability planning should begin from Day 1 of implementation (not afterthought). By Year 3-5, EQUILIBRIUM should be "business as usual" with minimal special effort required.

**Impact:** Sustained EQUILIBRIUM implementation (5+ years, high fidelity) means thousands of patients receive evidence-based, holistic depression care year after year - not just during a time-limited project.
