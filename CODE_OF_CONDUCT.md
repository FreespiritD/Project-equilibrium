# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of Project Equilibrium pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community focused on advancing medical education and patient care.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Respectful Communication**: Using welcoming and inclusive language
- **Professional Discourse**: Engaging in constructive professional discussions
- **Collaborative Spirit**: Working together toward shared goals
- **Open-mindedness**: Being receptive to different viewpoints and experiences
- **Empathy**: Showing consideration for others' perspectives and circumstances
- **Evidence-Based Discussion**: Grounding debates in evidence and data
- **Constructive Feedback**: Providing helpful, specific, and kind feedback
- **Accountability**: Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- **Focus on Mission**: Prioritizing the project's educational and clinical goals
- **Acknowledgment**: Recognizing and crediting others' contributions

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment**: Offensive comments, personal attacks, or unwelcome attention
- **Discrimination**: Prejudiced behavior based on personal characteristics
- **Disrespect**: Trolling, insulting comments, or derogatory language
- **Privacy Violations**: Sharing others' private information without consent
- **Professional Misconduct**: Behavior that violates professional standards
- **Intimidation**: Threats, stalking, or aggressive behavior
- **Disruption**: Deliberately derailing discussions or obstructing work
- **Inappropriate Content**: Sharing sexual, violent, or otherwise inappropriate material
- **Plagiarism**: Misrepresenting others' work as your own
- **Misrepresentation**: Falsifying credentials, data, or contributions

### Professional Context

Given this project's focus on medical education and clinical care:

- Maintain patient privacy and confidentiality in all discussions
- Adhere to professional standards of medical and educational practice
- Distinguish between evidence-based recommendations and personal opinions
- Respect institutional policies and regulatory requirements
- Acknowledge limitations of expertise
- Avoid conflicts of interest or disclose them appropriately

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Project Equilibrium spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and issue trackers
- Communication channels (forums, chat, email lists)
- Working group meetings and discussions
- Community events and conferences
- Social media when representing the project
- Any other project-related settings

It also applies when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders and maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

### Enforcement Authority

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to:
- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct
- Communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate
- Temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviors deemed inappropriate

## Reporting

### How to Report

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it promptly:

1. **Direct Contact**: Email project maintainers at [conduct@project-equilibrium.org]
2. **Anonymous Reporting**: Use the anonymous reporting form at [URL]
3. **Institutional Channels**: Contact your institutional representative if applicable

### What to Include

When reporting, please provide:
- Your contact information (if not anonymous)
- Description of the incident
- Date, time, and location (virtual or physical)
- Names of individuals involved
- Names of witnesses (if any)
- Any relevant documentation (screenshots, messages, etc.)
- Whether you've reported this elsewhere

### Confidentiality

All reports will be handled with discretion. Reporters' identities will be protected except where disclosure is required by law or necessary for investigation.

## Enforcement Process

### 1. Acknowledgment
Reports will be acknowledged within 48 hours.

### 2. Investigation
- Review of reported behavior and context
- Interviews with involved parties and witnesses
- Consultation with relevant experts if needed
- Timeline: 1-2 weeks for most cases

### 3. Decision
Enforcement actions may include:

#### Level 1: Correction
**Impact**: Minor inappropriate behavior or misunderstanding
**Action**: Private written warning with clarification of violation and expectations

#### Level 2: Warning
**Impact**: Single incident or series of minor violations
**Action**: Formal warning with consequences for continued behavior, including temporary restrictions on participation

#### Level 3: Temporary Suspension
**Impact**: Serious violation or pattern of inappropriate behavior
**Action**: Temporary ban from project spaces (typically 30-90 days)

#### Level 4: Permanent Ban
**Impact**: Severe violations, repeated offenses, or sustained inappropriate behavior
**Action**: Permanent removal from all project spaces and activities

### 4. Appeal
Individuals may appeal enforcement decisions by contacting the governance committee within 14 days of notification.

## Context-Specific Guidelines

### Academic Discussions
- Robust debate is encouraged within professional norms
- Disagree on ideas, not people
- Cite evidence and acknowledge uncertainty
- Respect different theoretical frameworks and methodologies

### Clinical Content
- Patient safety and privacy are paramount
- Defer to current evidence and best practices
- Acknowledge scope of practice limitations
- Respect institutional and regulatory contexts

### Implementation Discussions
- Value diverse institutional experiences
- Share both successes and failures
- Avoid blaming individuals or institutions
- Focus on learning and improvement

## Building a Positive Community

### We Encourage

- **Mentorship**: Supporting new contributors
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Teaching and learning from each other
- **Collaboration**: Working across disciplines and institutions
- **Innovation**: Proposing new ideas and approaches
- **Reflection**: Sharing lessons learned
- **Celebration**: Recognizing achievements and milestones

### Communication Best Practices

- Assume positive intent
- Ask clarifying questions
- Use "I" statements when expressing concerns
- Take breaks if discussions become heated
- Move to private channels for personal issues
- Acknowledge different communication styles and preferences
