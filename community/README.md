# Community

Spaces for community engagement, collaboration, and knowledge sharing.

## Structure

- **discussions/**: Community discussions, Q&A, and knowledge exchange
- **working-groups/**: Working group documentation, meeting notes, and collaborative spaces
- **events/**: Event planning, conference materials, and community gatherings

## Getting Involved

### Join Discussions
- Ask questions
- Share experiences
- Provide feedback
- Propose ideas
- Help others

### Working Groups
Current working groups:
- Curriculum Working Group
- Clinical Pathways Working Group
- Research Working Group
- Implementation Working Group
- Technical Working Group
- Communications Working Group
- Quality Working Group

See [GOVERNANCE.md](/GOVERNANCE.md) for details on working group structure and participation.

### Events
- Community calls (monthly)
- Working group meetings
- Annual conference
- Training workshops
- Webinar series

## Communication Channels

- GitHub Discussions: Primary forum for community interaction
- Mailing lists: Announcements and updates
- Working group channels: Focused collaboration
- Social media: Broader engagement
