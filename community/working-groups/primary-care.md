# Primary Care Working Group

## Purpose

Develop and refine holistic mental health clinical pathways, support GP practice implementation, and foster peer learning among primary care practitioners.

### How to Join

**Eligibility:**
- GPs and primary care practitioners

**To join:**
1. Complete [membership application](#)
2. Attend introductory meeting
3. Commit to participate actively

## Activities

### Core Functions

- **Clinical Pathway Development**: Create and refine clinical algorithms
- **Practice Support**: Peer support for implementing practices
- **Resource Curation**: Compile local and national resources
- **Quality Improvement**: Share QI approaches and outcomes
- **Research**: Practice-based implementation research
- **Advocacy**: Promote system changes needed for holistic care

### Meetings

**Monthly meetings**: Second Tuesday, 19:00-20:30 BST (GP-friendly evening time)
- Online via Zoom
- Recording available for those unable to attend live

**Annual conference**: June (1-day in-person)

### Subgroups

- **Depression Pathway**: Focus on depression protocols
- **Anxiety Pathway**: Focus on anxiety protocols
- **Social Prescribing**: Community resource integration
- **Payment Models**: Enhanced payment frameworks
