# Medical Education Working Group

## Purpose

Develop and refine the EQUILIBRIUM medical school curriculum, support implementation, and share best practices among medical educators.

## Membership

### Current Members
*Members to be added as working group forms*

### How to Join

**Eligibility:**
- Medical educators (faculty, curriculum leads)
- Medical education researchers
- Medical students (student representatives)
- Anyone with expertise in medical education

**To join:**
1. Complete [membership application](#)
2. Attend introductory meeting
3. Commit to participate in at least 50% of meetings

## Activities

### Core Functions

- **Curriculum Development**: Create and refine educational materials
- **Faculty Development**: Design and deliver training programs
- **Assessment**: Develop competency assessment tools
- **Implementation Support**: Support pilot and scale-up sites
- **Research**: Evaluate educational interventions
- **Dissemination**: Share findings and best practices

### Meetings

**Monthly meetings**: First Wednesday, 17:00-18:30 BST
- Online via Zoom
- Hybrid option at annual in-person meeting

**Annual in-person meeting**: September (coinciding with medical education conferences)

### Subgroups

- **Curriculum Content**: Detailed material development
- **Assessment & Evaluation**: Tools and frameworks
- **Faculty Development**: Training program design
- **Implementation Science**: Supporting adoption
