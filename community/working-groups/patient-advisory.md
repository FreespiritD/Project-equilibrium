# Patient Advisory Group

## Purpose

Ensure patient and lived experience perspectives shape all aspects of Project EQUILIBRIUM - from curriculum to clinical pathways to evaluation.

## Why Patient Voice Matters

Mental health care must be designed **with** patients, not just **for** patients. Your lived experience is expertise that complements clinical and research knowledge.

## Membership

### Current Members
*Members to be added as group forms*

### How to Join

**Who we're looking for:**
- People with lived experience of depression and/or anxiety
- Family members/carers of people with mental health conditions
- Patient advocates and representatives
- Diversity of ages, backgrounds, experiences, treatments
- UK-based (though international perspectives welcome)

**What we ask:**
- Willingness to share your perspective
- Commitment to attend meetings (flexibility for mental health needs)
- Ability to review materials and provide feedback
- Respectful collaboration with others

**No experience required!** We value your lived experience - that's the expertise we need.

**To join:**
1. Complete [expression of interest form](#)
2. Brief phone/video call with coordinator
3. Welcome meeting with group

## Activities

### What We Do

**Co-Design:**
- Review and feedback on curriculum materials
- Help design patient information materials
- Test decision aids and assessment tools
- Shape evaluation questions and methods
- Advise on patient recruitment and consent

**Consultation:**
- Respond to specific questions from working groups
- Review draft materials and provide feedback
- Share experiences relevant to project decisions

**Representation:**
- Patient representatives on steering committee
- Attend working group meetings
- Present at conferences and events
- Contribute to publications

**Advocacy:**
- Promote project to patient communities
- Share patient perspectives publicly
- Advocate for needed system changes

### Meetings

**Quarterly meetings**: Via Zoom (2 hours)
- Accessible format with breaks
- Materials circulated in advance
- Recording available

**Ad-hoc consultations**: As needed for specific questions

**Annual in-person meeting**: Optional 1-day gathering

## Current Focus Areas

### 2025 Priorities

1. **Patient Information Materials**
   - Review treatment options guide
   - Co-design decision aids
   - Develop FAQ for patients

2. **Clinical Pathway Input**
   - Ensure pathways reflect patient preferences
   - Review shared decision-making approaches
   - Advise on language and communication

3. **Evaluation Design**
   - What outcomes matter most to patients?
   - How to measure patient experience?
   - Accessible consent and feedback processes

4. **Student Engagement**
   - How should medical students learn from patient experience?
   - Patient involvement in teaching
