# Student Ambassador Program

## About the Program

The EQUILIBRIUM Student Ambassador Program empowers medical students to lead mental health education transformation at their institutions and contribute to the future of medical training.

## Why Become an Ambassador?

### Make an Impact
- Shape how mental health is taught
- Influence curriculum at your medical school
- Contribute to national education reform
- Improve care for future patients

### Develop Skills
- Leadership and advocacy
- Project management
- Public speaking and presentation
- Research and evaluation
- Peer education

### Build Your CV
- Leadership role in national initiative
- Project participation for portfolio
- Networking with healthcare leaders
- Potential for publications
- Recognition certificate
- Reference letters available

### Connect with Peers
- National network of student leaders
- Peer support and mentorship
- Annual gatherings and events
- Online community

## Ambassador Roles

### School Representative (1-2 per school)

**Responsibilities:**
- Represent student voice in EQUILIBRIUM implementation
- Gather student feedback on curriculum
- Support faculty in pilot implementation
- Organize peer learning events
- Share experiences with national network

**Time commitment**: 3-5 hours/month

### National Advocate (5-10 students)

**Responsibilities:**
- Present at conferences and events
- Write blog posts and articles
- Social media advocacy
- Media training and engagement
- Policy advocacy with student organizations

**Time commitment**: 5-10 hours/month

### Research Assistant (Variable)

**Responsibilities:**
- Support evaluation activities
- Data collection at pilot sites
- Literature reviews
- Student outcome research
- Co-authorship opportunities

**Time commitment**: 10-20 hours/month (may be paid)

## How to Apply

### Eligibility

- Currently enrolled medical student (any year)
- UK medical school
- Passion for mental health education
- Commitment to program (minimum 1 year)

### Application Process

1. **Submit application** (opens September annually)
   - Personal statement (500 words)
   - CV
   - Reference from faculty member

2. **Video interview** (selected applicants)

3. **Selection** (October)

4. **Onboarding** (November)

**Next application deadline**: 30 September 2025

## FAQs

**Q: I'm a first year student - can I apply?**
A: Yes! We welcome students from all years. Different perspectives are valuable.

**Q: I'm not at a pilot school - can I still join?**
A: Yes! Ambassadors at non-pilot schools help prepare for future implementation.

**Q: What if I have exams or placements?**
A: We're flexible. Take breaks when needed. Minimum engagement expected, but we understand student life.

**Q: Do I get paid?**
A: Most ambassador activities are voluntary. Research assistant roles may be paid. All expenses reimbursed.

**Q: Will this help my foundation application?**
A: Yes! Leadership, project work, and commitment to education are valued. Reference letters available.
