# Project EQUILIBRIUM Office Hours

## About Office Hours

Weekly drop-in sessions where you can ask questions, get guidance, and connect with the project team and other contributors.

**No appointment needed – just join!**

## Schedule

### General Office Hours

**When**: Every Thursday, 16:00-17:00 BST

**Where**: Zoom (link sent to mailing list weekly)

**Who**: Open to all – potential contributors, current contributors, pilot sites, anyone interested

**Format**:
- Informal Q&A
- Get help with contributions
- Meet other contributors
- Learn about current projects

### Specialized Office Hours

#### Medical Educators

**When**: First Wednesday of month, 17:00-18:00 BST

**Focus**: Curriculum development, teaching methods, faculty training

**Host**: Medical Education Working Group Chair

#### GPs and Clinicians

**When**: Second Tuesday of month, 19:00-20:00 BST (GP-friendly evening time)

**Focus**: Clinical pathways, implementation, practice questions

**Host**: Clinical Lead

#### Researchers

**When**: Third Friday of month, 14:00-15:00 BST

**Focus**: Research collaborations, data access, publications

**Host**: Research Lead

#### Students

**When**: Last Thursday of month, 18:00-19:00 BST

**Focus**: Student ambassadors, curriculum feedback, getting involved

**Host**: Student Ambassador Coordinator

## What to Bring

- Questions about the project

- Desire to connect with others

## Office Hours Guidelines

### We Welcome

- All questions (no "stupid questions"!)
- Ideas and suggestions
- Constructive feedback
- Sharing challenges
- Requests for help

### Please

- Be respectful of others' time
- Stay on topic
- Follow Code of Conduct
- Mute when not speaking
- Use chat for links/references

### Topics for Other Venues

- Detailed code reviews (schedule dedicated time)
- Long presentations (propose at working group)
- Private/confidential matters (email directly)
- Complaints (use appropriate channels)
