# Conflict of Interest (COI) Disclosure Form

**Project EQUILIBRIUM Contributor Declaration**

## Purpose

Project EQUILIBRIUM maintains integrity through transparent conflict of interest disclosure. This form helps identify potential conflicts that could bias project contributions.

## Who Must Complete This Form

All contributors to Project EQUILIBRIUM must complete this form:
- Steering Committee members
- Working Group chairs and members
- Content developers (curriculum, clinical pathways, evidence reviews)
- Researchers and evaluators
- Anyone contributing substantially to project materials

## Disclosure Requirements

Please disclose any financial or professional relationships that could represent a conflict of interest, including:

### Financial Interests

**1. Employment**
- Current employer(s)
- Position/role
- If pharmaceutical or medical device company

**2. Consulting**
- Companies you consult for
- Nature of consulting
- Approximate annual income from each

**3. Stock/Equity**
- Companies in which you hold stock/equity
- If >$10,000 value in single company
- If pharmaceutical, biotech, or healthcare companies

**4. Honoraria/Speaker Fees**
- Organizations paying for speaking engagements
- Approximate amounts in past 2 years

**5. Research Funding**
- Sources of research funding
- Amounts and timelines
- If pharmaceutical industry funded

**6. Advisory Boards**
- Companies/organizations where you serve on advisory boards
- Paid or unpaid
- Nature of organization

**7. Patents/Royalties**
- Related patents or patent applications
- Products generating royalties

### Professional Relationships

**8. Leadership Positions**
- Professional organizations
- Advocacy groups
- Industry associations

**9. Family Interests**
- Immediate family members with relevant financial interests
- Spouse, partner, or dependent children

## COI Policy

### Restrictions Based on Disclosures

**Contributors with pharmaceutical industry funding >£10,000/year:**
- ❌ Cannot contribute to evidence reviews or synthesis
- ❌ Cannot develop curriculum content on medications or interventions
- ❌ Cannot contribute to policy recommendations
- ✅ Can contribute technical/coding improvements
- ✅ Can participate in general discussions (disclosed COI noted)

**All contributors with COIs:**
- Must disclose COI when contributing
- COI noted on relevant materials
- Recuse from decisions where conflict exists

### Review Process

- COI forms reviewed by Governance Committee
- Contributors notified of any restrictions
- Concerns or disputes resolved by independent review
