# Student Feedback on EQUILIBRIUM Curriculum

## Why Your Feedback Matters

As medical students, you are experiencing the EQUILIBRIUM curriculum firsthand. Your feedback is essential for:
- Improving curriculum content and delivery
- Refining teaching methods
- Identifying gaps or areas needing more support
- Demonstrating student outcomes
- Shaping scale-up to other schools

## How to Provide Feedback

### Quick Feedback (Anytime)

**Online form** (anonymous option available): [Feedback form](#)

**Topics:**
- Specific teaching sessions
- Assessment experiences
- Resources and materials
- Clinical placements
- Overall experience

### Regular Feedback Opportunities

**End of term surveys**: Comprehensive feedback on term's teaching

**Focus groups**: In-depth discussion (2-3 times per year)

**Student-staff meetings**: Formal feedback forums

### Urgent Concerns

If you have concerns needing immediate attention:
- Contact your Student Ambassador
- Email student support: studentsupport@projectequilibrium.org
- Speak with year lead or personal tutor

## What We Ask About

### Content
- Is content at right level?
- Is evidence base clear?
- Are examples relevant?
- What's missing?
- What's unnecessary?

### Teaching
- Are teaching methods effective?
- Do activities engage you?
- Is time allocated appropriately?
- Are faculty knowledgeable and approachable?

### Assessment
- Do assessments fairly test learning?
- Are expectations clear?
- Is feedback helpful?
- Are you adequately prepared?

### Resources
- Are materials accessible?
- Is online content helpful?
- Do you have enough practice opportunities?
- What additional resources would help?

### Clinical Application
- Can you apply learning in placements?
- Do supervisors support practice?
- Are patients receptive?
- Do you feel competent?

## Your Feedback in Action

We're committed to acting on feedback:

**Monthly**: Review all feedback submitted
**Termly**: Implement quick fixes and improvements
**Annually**: Major curriculum revisions based on accumulated feedback
**Transparent**: Share what changes resulted from feedback

## Feedback FAQs

**Q: Is feedback really anonymous?**
A: Yes, if you use anonymous form. Named feedback kept confidential unless action required.

**Q: Will my feedback affect my grades?**
A: No. Feedback is separate from assessment. Constructive criticism is valued.

**Q: What if I don't like something but others do?**
A: All perspectives valued. We look for patterns but also consider minority views.

**Q: How do I know my feedback was read?**
A: "You said, we did" reports published each term showing feedback themes and actions.
