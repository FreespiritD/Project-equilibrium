# EQUILIBRIUM Curriculum: Detailed Learning Objectives

## Overview

This document provides detailed, assessable learning objectives for the EQUILIBRIUM curriculum, organized by year and module. These objectives operationalize the high-level learning outcomes into specific competencies that students must demonstrate to graduate.

## Learning Objective Framework

### Taxonomy
Objectives are written using **Bloom's Taxonomy** (revised):
- **Remember**: Recall facts and basic concepts
- **Understand**: Explain ideas or concepts
- **Apply**: Use information in new situations
- **Analyze**: Draw connections among ideas
- **Evaluate**: Justify a decision or course of action
- **Create**: Produce new or original work

### Assessment Methods
- **Written exams**: Knowledge and understanding
- **OSCEs**: Skills demonstration
- **Clinical assessments**: Real-world application
- **Portfolios**: Reflection and attitudes
- **Case presentations**: Analysis and evaluation

### GMC Alignment
All objectives map to **Tomorrow's Doctors** outcomes and are tagged accordingly.

## Progressive Development Across Years

### Year 1: KNOW
- **Knowledge**: Evidence base, mechanisms, basic science
- **Level**: Remember, Understand
- **Assessment**: Written exams, essays

### Year 2: UNDERSTAND HOW
- **Skills**: How to deliver interventions, formulate cases
- **Level**: Apply, Analyze
- **Assessment**: OSCEs, skills demonstrations

### Years 3-4: DO
- **Practice**: Real patients, supervised clinical application
- **Level**: Apply, Evaluate, Create
- **Assessment**: Workplace-based assessments, portfolios

### Graduation: MASTERY
- **Competency**: Independent, safe, evidence-based practice
- **Level**: All Bloom's levels integrated
- **Assessment**: Summative OSCEs, clinical exams, portfolio

## Assessment Blueprint

### Written Exams
- **Year 1**: 15% EQUILIBRIUM content (neuroscience, nutrition, exercise, sleep, psychology, social)
- **Year 2**: 15% EQUILIBRIUM content (psychotherapy, MI, formulation, mechanisms)
- **Years 3-4**: Integrated into clinical exams

### OSCEs
- **Year 1**: 1-2 stations (e.g., explain exercise to patient, take dietary history)
- **Year 2**: 2 stations (e.g., behavioral activation, shared decision-making)
- **Year 3**: 2 stations (e.g., assess and manage depression, prescribe holistically)
- **Year 4**: 2-3 stations (includes required competency stations)

### Portfolio
