# Curriculum

Educational materials and resources organized by training year and function.

## Structure

- **year-1/**: First-year curriculum, foundational content, and introductory materials
- **year-2/**: Second-year curriculum, intermediate skills, and clinical integration
- **year-3-4/**: Advanced curriculum for years 3 and 4, specialty rotations, and capstone experiences
- **assessment/**: Assessment tools, rubrics, evaluation instruments, and outcome measures
- **faculty-training/**: Faculty development materials, teaching guides, and educator resources

## Using These Materials

### For Faculty
1. Review learning objectives for each year
2. Adapt materials to your institutional context
3. Complete relevant faculty training modules
4. Implement assessment tools
5. Share feedback and improvements

### For Students
Materials are designed to support your learning journey through progressive skill development and clinical integration.
