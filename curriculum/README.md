# Project EQUILIBRIUM Medical Curriculum

## Overview

The EQUILIBRIUM curriculum represents a transformative approach to medical education, integrating evidence-based holistic interventions throughout the standard medical school curriculum. This ensures that future doctors are competent in prescribing exercise, diet, sleep interventions, psychotherapy, and social support alongside traditional pharmacological approaches.

## Vision

**To graduate doctors who can confidently offer patients a full range of evidence-based treatment options, prescribing appropriately and reducing reliance on medication when equally effective alternatives exist.**

## Core Principles

1. **Evidence-Based**: All content grounded in Grade A/B evidence
2. **Integrated**: Holistic approaches embedded throughout, not separate
3. **Practical**: Skills-focused with supervised practice opportunities
4. **Progressive**: Builds from basic science → clinical application → mastery
5. **Assessed**: Competencies formally tested (OSCEs, exams, portfolios)
6. **Sustainable**: Faculty training ensures long-term implementation

## Curriculum Structure

### Year 1: Foundations (Pre-Clinical)
**Focus**: Basic science foundations for holistic interventions

- **Module 1**: Neuroscience of Wellbeing (10 weeks)
- **Module 2**: Nutritional Medicine (6 weeks)
- **Module 3**: Movement as Medicine (6 weeks)
- **Module 4**: Sleep and Circadian Biology (4 weeks)
- **Module 5**: Psychology of Human Experience (8 weeks)
- **Module 6**: Social Determinants of Health (6 weeks)

**Total**: 40 weeks integrated into existing Year 1 curriculum

### Year 2: Mechanisms and Interventions (Pre-Clinical)
**Focus**: Understanding therapeutic mechanisms and intervention delivery

- **Module 7**: Evidence-Based Psychotherapy (10 weeks)
- **Module 8**: Mind-Body Practices (6 weeks)
- **Module 9**: Biopsychosocial Formulation (8 weeks)

**Total**: 24 weeks integrated into existing Year 2 curriculum

### Years 3-4: Clinical Application (Clinical)
**Focus**: Supervised practice in real patient care

- **Depression Module** (4 weeks clinical rotation)
- **Anxiety Module** (4 weeks clinical rotation)
- **Trauma & PTSD Module** (2 weeks clinical rotation)
- **Integrated Clinical Cases** (Throughout clinical years)

**Total**: 10+ weeks dedicated clinical experience, plus integration throughout all clinical rotations

## Learning Outcomes

By graduation, students will be able to:

### Knowledge
1. **Explain** the evidence base for lifestyle and psychological interventions
2. **Describe** biological mechanisms (neuroplasticity, inflammation, gut-brain axis, social neuroscience)
3. **Compare** effectiveness of interventions vs. medication
4. **Identify** inappropriate prescribing patterns

### Skills
5. **Assess** patients holistically (mood, sleep, activity, diet, social, stress)
6. **Prescribe** exercise, diet, sleep interventions using evidence-based protocols
7. **Deliver** brief psychological interventions
8. **Practice** motivational interviewing and shared decision-making
9. **Refer** appropriately to therapy and community resources

### Attitudes
10. **Value** non-pharmacological approaches as first-line treatments
11. **Demonstrate** patient-centered, collaborative approach
12. **Commit** to guideline-concordant prescribing
13. **Reflect** on own wellbeing practices

## Implementation Model

### Integration, Not Addition
- **Embedded** in existing modules (not separate)
- **Replaces** outdated content
- **Examples**:
  - Year 1 Neuroscience: Add neuroplasticity (remove outdated neuroanatomy)
  - Year 2 Pharmacology: Add comparative effectiveness
  - Year 3 Psychiatry: Add lifestyle prescription skills

### Spiral Curriculum
- **Year 1**: Basic science (mechanisms)
- **Year 2**: Evidence and interventions (how-to)
- **Years 3-4**: Clinical application (practice)

### Flipped Classroom
- **Pre-class**: Videos, readings (knowledge)
- **In-class**: Discussion, skills practice (application)
- **Clinical**: Real patients (mastery)

## Assessment Strategy

### Formative (Learning)
- Weekly quizzes
- Peer teaching
- Self-reflection journals
- Practice OSCEs

### Summative (Grading)
- Written exams (~15% EQUILIBRIUM content)
- OSCEs (2+ EQUILIBRIUM stations per year)
- Clinical assessments
- Portfolio

### Competency Requirements
- Must demonstrate lifestyle prescription competency to graduate
- Cannot proceed to clinical years without passing EQUILIBRIUM OSCEs
