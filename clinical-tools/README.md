# Clinical Tools

Evidence-based tools for clinical practice, patient care, and decision support for mental health in primary care.

## Structure

- **pathways/**: Clinical pathways for depression, anxiety, and mixed presentations
- **assessment-tools/**: Validated screening and monitoring instruments (PHQ-9, GAD-7, etc.)
- **patient-materials/**: Educational materials and self-help resources for patients
- **decision-aids/**: Shared decision-making tools for treatment options
- **ehr-templates/**: Electronic health record templates and documentation tools

## Project EQUILIBRIUM Clinical Approach

### Core Principles

1. **Holistic Assessment**: Biopsychosocial understanding of mental health presentations
2. **Evidence-Based Options**: Full range of interventions with demonstrated effectiveness
3. **Shared Decision-Making**: Patient preferences and values integrated into care planning
4. **Stratified Care**: Matching intervention intensity to severity and need
5. **Outcome Monitoring**: Regular assessment and adjustment based on response

### Clinical Pathways

EQUILIBRIUM provides comprehensive pathways for:

**Depression Pathway**
- Mild depression: Lifestyle and psychological interventions primary
- Moderate depression: Psychological therapy + lifestyle, medication if preferred/needed
- Severe depression: Medication + psychological + lifestyle support

**Anxiety Pathway**
- Generalized anxiety disorder
- Panic disorder
- Social anxiety disorder
- Mixed presentations

**Mixed Anxiety-Depression**
- Common primary care presentation
- Integrated assessment and management

Each pathway includes:
- ✓ Holistic assessment guidance
- ✓ Risk stratification
- ✓ Treatment algorithms
- ✓ Shared decision-making tools
- ✓ Monitoring protocols
- ✓ Safety netting and escalation criteria

### Intervention Options

**Psychological:**
- CBT techniques and behavioral activation
- Problem-solving therapy
- Mindfulness-based approaches
- IAPT referral pathways

**Lifestyle:**
- Exercise prescription
- Nutrition guidance
- Sleep interventions
- Stress management

**Social:**
- Social prescribing
- Community resources
- Peer support

**Pharmacological:**
- Evidence-based prescribing when indicated
- Appropriate medication selection
- Monitoring and review protocols

## Important Clinical Notes

### Safety

- **Risk assessment** integrated into all pathways
- **Safety netting** protocols for deterioration
- **Escalation criteria** clearly defined
- **Emergency pathways** specified
- **Safeguarding** considerations included

### Adaptability

- Pathways provide framework, not rigid protocol
- **Clinical judgment** essential
- **Patient context** must be considered
- **Local resources** will vary
- **Cultural adaptation** important

### Scope

These tools are designed for:
- ✓ Common mental health presentations in primary care
- ✓ Adults (18-65)
- ✓ Mild-to-moderate severity primarily
- ✓ UK NHS context (adaptable internationally)

**Not designed for:**
- ✗ Severe mental illness requiring specialist care
- ✗ Complex comorbidities beyond GP scope
- ✗ Children and adolescents (separate resources needed)
- ✗ Crisis presentations (emergency protocols apply)
