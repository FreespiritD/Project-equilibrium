# Biopsychosocial Assessment Template

**Version**: 2.0
**Last Updated**: October 2025
**Purpose**: Comprehensive holistic assessment for depression, anxiety, and related presentations

---

## Instructions for Use

This template guides a systematic biopsychosocial assessment. It should take 10-15 minutes for initial assessment.

**Not every question needs to be asked verbatim** - use clinical judgment to explore relevant areas based on presentation.

**Document key positive and negative findings** in each domain.

---

## BIOLOGICAL FACTORS

### Sleep

**Screen**: "How has your sleep been?"

**If disturbed, assess**:
- **Difficulty falling asleep?** (sleep onset latency)
  - How long does it take to fall asleep? (>30 min = insomnia)
- **Waking during the night?** (sleep maintenance)
  - How many times? How long awake?
- **Early morning awakening?** (terminal insomnia)
  - What time do you wake? Can you get back to sleep?
- **Total sleep time**: "On average, how many hours of actual sleep do you get per night?"
- **Sleep quality**: "Do you feel rested when you wake up?"
- **Daytime impact**: Fatigue, sleepiness, concentration affected?

**Assess for sleep disorders**:
- **Sleep apnea**: Snoring? Witnessed pauses in breathing? Gasping? Daytime sleepiness?
  - If suspected → Refer for sleep study
- **Restless legs**: Uncomfortable sensations in legs at night, urge to move, worse when resting?

**Sleep habits** (see `sleep-quality-assessment.md` for detailed tool):
- Bedtime and wake time (consistent or variable?)
- Screen use before bed
- Caffeine timing (coffee, tea, energy drinks)
- Alcohol before bed
- Bedroom environment (light, noise, temperature)

**Document**:
- Sleep onset latency: ___ min
- Total sleep time: ___ hours
- Sleep efficiency (if calculated): ___%
- Sleep disorder suspected: Yes / No (specify: _______)

### Nutrition and Diet

**Screen**: "Can you tell me about your typical diet?"

**Dietary patterns**:
- **Meal frequency**: "How many meals do you eat per day?"
  - Skipping meals? (blood sugar instability can worsen mood)
- **Diet quality**: "What does a typical day of eating look like?"
  - Fruits and vegetables (servings/day)
  - Processed foods (fast food, ready meals)
  - Sugar intake (sweets, sugary drinks)
- **Mediterranean diet components**:
  - Fish (oily fish like salmon, mackerel?)
  - Nuts, seeds
  - Olive oil
  - Whole grains
  - Legumes (beans, lentils)

**Use brief screener**: `nutrition-screener.md`

**Nutritional deficiencies** (if suspected from history):
- **Vitamin D**: Lack of sun exposure, darker skin, older adult?
- **B12**: Vegan diet, older adult, on metformin or PPIs?
- **Folate**: Poor diet, alcohol use?
- **Iron**: Menstruation, vegetarian, fatigue?
- **Omega-3**: Low fish intake?

**Consider testing if**:
- Severe fatigue
- Poor dietary variety
- Risk factors present

**Appetite changes** (for depression assessment):
- Increased or decreased?
- Weight change? (kg/pounds, timeframe)

**Document**:
- Diet quality: Poor / Fair / Good / Excellent
- Mediterranean diet adherence: Low / Moderate / High
- Nutritional deficiency suspected: Yes / No (specify: _______)
- Referral to dietitian needed: Yes / No

---

### Physical Activity and Exercise

**Screen**: "How physically active are you?"

**Use assessment tool**: `exercise-assessment.md`

**Quantify**:
- **Type**: Walking, running, cycling, swimming, gym, sports, active job?
- **Frequency**: Days per week
- **Duration**: Minutes per session
- **Intensity**:
  - Light (can sing while doing it - slow walking)
  - Moderate (can talk but not sing - brisk walking)
  - Vigorous (can't talk - running)

**Calculate total weekly moderate activity** (min/week):
- UK guideline: 150 min/week moderate OR 75 min/week vigorous
- Depression treatment dose: Similar (150 min/week associated with clinical improvement)

**Barriers to exercise**:
- "What stops you from being more active?"
  - Common: Time, motivation, fatigue, pain, cost

**Sedentary time**:
- "How many hours per day do you spend sitting?" (work, TV, computer)
- Prolonged sitting is independent risk factor for poor mental health

**Document**:
- Current activity level: ___ min/week moderate (or) ___ min/week vigorous
- Meets 150 min/week guideline: Yes / No
- Barriers: ______________________
- Exercise prescription given: Yes / No (see treatment plan)

### Substance Use

#### Alcohol

**Screen**: "How much alcohol do you drink?"

**Quantify**:
- Type (beer, wine, spirits)
- Frequency (days per week)
- Amount per occasion (units)
  - **1 unit** = Half pint normal beer, small glass wine (125ml), single spirit
  - **NICE**: <14 units/week spread over ≥3 days

**AUDIT-C** (brief screen):
1. How often do you drink alcohol? (Never to Daily)
2. How many units on a typical day? (1-2 to 10+)
3. How often 6+ units on one occasion? (Never to Daily)
- Score ≥5: Possible alcohol use disorder → Full AUDIT

**Pattern**:
- Binge drinking (≥6 units in one session)?
- Daily drinking?
- Morning drinking?
- Using alcohol to cope with mood/sleep?

**Impact**:
- Alcohol affects mood (depressant, rebound anxiety)
- Disrupts sleep architecture
- Interferes with antidepressant efficacy

**Document**:
- Units per week: ____
- Exceeds guidelines (>14 units/week): Yes / No
- Hazardous drinking (AUDIT-C ≥5): Yes / No
- Alcohol as self-medication for mood/sleep: Yes / No

#### Caffeine

**Screen**: "How much coffee, tea, or energy drinks do you have each day?"

**Quantify** (mg caffeine):
- Coffee: ~100mg per cup (instant), ~150mg (brewed)
- Tea: ~50mg per cup
- Energy drinks: 80-160mg per can
- Cola: ~40mg per can

**Total daily caffeine**: _____mg

**Timing**: "When do you have your last caffeinated drink?"
- After 2pm → likely interfering with sleep

**Anxiety**: High caffeine (>400mg/day) can trigger/worsen anxiety and panic

**Recommend**: <200mg/day, none after 2pm (especially if insomnia or anxiety)

**Document**:
- Caffeine intake: ___mg/day (Low <100, Moderate 100-400, High >400)
- Afternoon/evening caffeine: Yes / No (affects sleep)
- Reduce caffeine advised: Yes / No

#### Other Substances

**Screen**:
- **Cannabis**: Frequency? Impact on mood (acute relief, chronic worsening)
- **Tobacco/vaping**: Packs per day? (nicotine stimulant, affects sleep)
- **Recreational drugs**: Cocaine, MDMA, etc. (mood instability)

**Document**:
- Substance use (specify type, frequency): ______________________

### Medical Comorbidities

**Screen**: "Do you have any ongoing medical conditions?"

**Common conditions affecting mental health**:
- **Endocrine**: Hypothyroidism (fatigue, low mood), diabetes (blood sugar swings)
- **Cardiac**: Post-MI depression (30% develop depression)
- **Respiratory**: COPD, asthma (chronic stress, breathlessness anxiety)
- **Neurological**: Parkinson's, MS, stroke, dementia
- **Chronic pain**: Fibromyalgia, arthritis (bidirectional with depression)
- **Gastrointestinal**: IBS (gut-brain axis)
- **Cancer**: High depression rates

**Review medications**:
- **Depressogenic**: Beta-blockers, corticosteroids, isotretinoin, interferon
- **Interactions**: NSAIDs + SSRIs (GI bleed risk), anticoagulants + SSRIs

**Document**:
- Relevant medical conditions: ______________________
- Medications potentially affecting mood: ______________________

## PSYCHOLOGICAL FACTORS

### Mood Symptoms

**Use validated tools**:
- **Depression**: PHQ-9 (see `phq-9-scoring.md`)
- **Anxiety**: GAD-7

**Core symptoms assessed**:
- **Mood**: Low, sad, empty, hopeless
- **Anhedonia**: Loss of interest, pleasure in activities previously enjoyed
- **Energy**: Fatigue, everything feels effortful
- **Concentration**: Difficulty focusing, making decisions

**Duration**: "How long have you been feeling this way?"
- >2 weeks for depression diagnosis

**Previous episodes**: "Have you ever felt like this before?"
- Recurrent depression → longer treatment, higher relapse risk

---

### Cognitive Patterns

**Explore thinking styles**:

**Negative automatic thoughts**:
- "What kinds of thoughts go through your mind when you're feeling low/anxious?"
- Common themes:
  - **Self-critical**: "I'm useless, I'm a failure"
  - **Hopelessness**: "Things will never get better"
  - **Catastrophizing**: "This will end in disaster"
  - **Mind-reading**: "Everyone thinks I'm stupid"

**Rumination**:
- "Do you find yourself dwelling on things, unable to let thoughts go?"

**Worry** (anxiety):
- "Do you worry excessively about things?"
- "Can you control the worry?"

**Document**:
- Predominant thinking patterns: ______________________
  - Negative self-view: Yes / No
  - Hopelessness: Yes / No
  - Excessive worry: Yes / No
  - Rumination: Yes / No

---

### Coping Strategies

**Ask**: "How do you cope when you're feeling low/anxious?"

**Adaptive coping** (helpful):
- Exercise, talking to friends/family, hobbies, mindfulness, problem-solving

**Maladaptive coping** (unhelpful):
- Avoidance, isolation, substance use, self-harm, excessive reassurance-seeking

**Behavioral changes** (in depression):
- Withdrawal from activities
- Reduced socializing
- Staying in bed
- Procrastination
- Loss of productivity

**Document**:
- Adaptive coping strategies: ______________________
- Maladaptive coping strategies: ______________________
- Behavioral withdrawal: Yes / No (describe: _______)

---

### Trauma and Adverse Experiences

**Screen sensitively**: "Have you experienced any traumatic or very distressing events in your life?"

**Types**:
- Childhood trauma (abuse, neglect, household dysfunction)
- Adult trauma (assault, accidents, combat, bereavement)
- Ongoing trauma (domestic violence, persecution)

**PTSD symptoms** (if trauma history):
- Re-experiencing (flashbacks, nightmares)
- Avoidance (of reminders)
- Hyperarousal (startle, hypervigilance, irritability)
- Negative thoughts/mood

**Impact on current presentation**:
- Trauma-related depression may need trauma-focused therapy (EMDR, TF-CBT)

**Document**:
- Trauma history: Yes / No (specify if disclosed: _______)
- PTSD symptoms: Yes / No
- Trauma-focused therapy needed: Yes / No

---

### Meaning, Purpose, and Values

**Explore**:
- "What gives your life meaning or purpose?"
  - Work, relationships, hobbies, faith, creativity?
- "Have you lost interest in things that used to matter to you?" (values-behavior gap in depression)

**Existential concerns**:
- Feeling life is meaningless, pointless (common in severe depression)

**Document**:
- Sources of meaning: ______________________
- Loss of connection to values: Yes / No

## SOCIAL FACTORS

### Relationships and Social Support

**Screen**: "Tell me about the important people in your life."

**Assess**:
- **Marital/partner status**: Married, partnered, single, divorced, widowed
  - **Relationship quality**: Supportive? Conflictual? Abusive?
- **Family**: Close to family? Family support or family stress?
- **Friendships**: Close friends? How often see/talk to friends?
- **Social network size**: Isolated vs. well-connected

**Loneliness**:
- "Do you feel lonely?"
- Loneliness is distinct from being alone (can be lonely in a crowd, can be alone and not lonely)
- Loneliness doubles depression risk

**Social withdrawal** (symptom of depression):
- "Have you been avoiding people or social situations?"

**Document**:
- Partner status: Single / Partnered / Married / Divorced / Widowed
- Partner relationship quality: Supportive / Neutral / Conflictual
- Close friendships: None / Few (1-2) / Several (3+)
- Social isolation: Yes / No
- Loneliness: Yes / No

---

### Work and Occupation

**Employment status**: Employed / Unemployed / Retired / Student / Homemaker / Off sick

**If employed**:
- **Job satisfaction**: Enjoys work? Stressful?
- **Work-life balance**: Overworking? Long hours?
- **Job security**: Stable or at risk?
- **Sick leave**: Currently off work due to mental health?

**If unemployed**:
- **Duration**: How long unemployed?
- **Financial impact**: Struggling financially?
- **Seeking work**: Actively looking? Barriers?

**Occupational stress**:
- High demands, low control (classic risk factor for depression)
- Bullying, harassment
- Lack of recognition, reward

**Impact of mental health on work**:
- Concentration, productivity affected?
- Sickness absence?
- Risk of job loss?

**Document**:
- Employment status: ______________________
- Work stress: None / Low / Moderate / High
- Currently off work due to mental health: Yes / No
- Occupational issues contributing to presentation: Yes / No (specify: _______)

### Financial Stress

**Screen**: "Are you experiencing any financial difficulties?"

**Assess**:
- Debt (credit cards, loans, overdraft)
- Inability to pay bills (rent, utilities, food)
- Bankruptcy, repossession risk
- Benefits issues (ESA, UC applications)

**Impact**:
- Financial stress is major contributor to anxiety and depression
- Poverty is a social determinant of mental health

**Signposting**:
- Citizens Advice Bureau
- Debt counseling services
- Benefits advice

**Document**:
- Financial stress: None / Mild / Moderate / Severe
- Benefits advice needed: Yes / No

## RISK ASSESSMENT

### Suicide Risk

**Always ask directly**: "Have you had thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life?"

**If yes, assess**:
- **Frequency**: Fleeting thoughts vs. constant
- **Intensity**: Passive ("wish I wouldn't wake up") vs. active ("plan to end life")
- **Intent**: Do you intend to act on thoughts?
- **Plan**: Specific method? When? Where?
- **Access to means**: Medications, ligatures, firearms?
- **Previous attempts**: History of self-harm or suicide attempts?
- **Protective factors**: Reasons to live? Future plans? Relationships?

**Classify risk**:
- **Low**: Thoughts, no plan, strong protective factors
- **Moderate**: Plans but no immediate intent, some protective factors
- **High**: Plan + intent + means, few protective factors, previous attempts, impulsivity

**Action**:
- **Low**: Safety plan (crisis numbers, remove means), close follow-up (within 1 week), consider crisis team referral
- **Moderate**: Same-day mental health assessment (crisis team, CMHT)
- **High**: Emergency referral (psychiatric liaison, A&E, Section if refuses)

**Document**:
- Suicidal ideation: None / Passive / Active with plan
- Suicide risk level: Low / Moderate / High
- Action taken: ______________________

---

### Self-Neglect

**Assess**: Eating? Drinking fluids? Washing? Basic self-care?

**Severe depression can impair** ability to care for self

**Action**: If severe → safeguarding, crisis team, consider admission

---

### Safeguarding (Vulnerable Adults and Children)

**Consider**: Are there vulnerable people at risk due to patient's mental health?

**Vulnerable adults**: Elderly parents, disabled partner
**Children**: Parenting capacity affected?

**Domestic violence**: "Do you feel safe at home?"

**Action**: Follow local safeguarding protocols if concerns

**Document**:
- Safeguarding concerns: Yes / No (specify: _______)
- Referral to safeguarding team: Yes / No

## BIOPSYCHOSOCIAL FORMULATION

**Synthesize findings** into integrated understanding:

### Biological Contributing Factors
- E.g., "Poor sleep (4-5 hours/night), low physical activity (<30 min/week), high caffeine intake (500mg/day), possible vitamin D deficiency"

### Psychological Contributing Factors
- E.g., "Negative self-critical thinking ('I'm a failure'), behavioral withdrawal (stopped seeing friends, quit hobbies), loss of meaning and purpose"

### Social Contributing Factors
- E.g., "Job loss 6 months ago, financial stress, social isolation, relationship conflict"

## TREATMENT PLAN

Based on biopsychosocial formulation, develop multi-component treatment plan addressing all domains:

**Biological interventions**:
- Exercise prescription (see `exercise-assessment.md`)
- Sleep hygiene / CBT-I (see `pathways/insomnia-pathway.md`)
- Dietary advice / Mediterranean diet (see `patient-materials/mediterranean-diet-guide.pdf`)
- Reduce caffeine / alcohol
- Medication (if indicated - see `pathways/depression-pathway.md` for severity-guided approach)

**Psychological interventions**:
- IAPT referral (low-intensity or high-intensity therapy)
- Self-help resources (guided self-help, online CBT)
- Cognitive-behavioral techniques (brief interventions in primary care)

**Social interventions**:
- Social prescribing (link worker referral)
- Addressing financial stress (Citizens Advice, benefits support)
- Work support (fit note, occupational health, graded return to work)

**Safety plan**:
- Follow-up interval (2-4 weeks depending on severity)
- Crisis numbers (GP, mental health crisis line, Samaritans)
- Risk management (if suicidal ideation)
