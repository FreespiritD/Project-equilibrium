## Using These References

### For Academic Publications

All references follow standard academic citation formats (APA 7th edition in markdown documents, BibTeX in .bib file).

### For Clinical Materials

Simplified citations are used in patient-facing documents, with full references available here.

### For Presentations

Key references are highlighted in each systematic review document under "Key References" sections.

## Adding New References

When adding new evidence documents:

1. **Add to bibliography.bib** in BibTeX format
2. **Cite in markdown** using Author (Year) format
3. **Include in Key References** section of relevant document
4. **Update evidence tables** (when created)

## Quality Standards

All included studies meet minimum quality standards:
- Peer-reviewed publications
- RCTs for intervention effectiveness
- Systematic reviews/meta-analyses prioritized
- GRADE assessment for strength of evidence
- Cochrane reviews given priority (gold standard)

## Evidence Grading

**Grade A** (Strong recommendation, high-quality evidence):
- Multiple high-quality RCTs or systematic reviews
- Consistent findings
- Large effect sizes or clear clinical benefit
- Low risk of bias

**Grade B** (Moderate recommendation, moderate-quality evidence):
- Some high-quality evidence
- Some inconsistency or limitations
- Moderate effect sizes
- Moderate risk of bias

**Grade C** (Weak recommendation, low-quality evidence):
- Limited high-quality evidence
- Inconsistent findings
- Small effect sizes or unclear benefit
- Higher risk of bias
