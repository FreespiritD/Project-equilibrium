# Patient Stories

## About Patient Stories

Real experiences from people who have faced depression and anxiety. These stories help:
- Others feel less alone
- Educate future doctors about patient perspectives
- Improve mental health care
- Advocate for better services

## Read Stories

*Stories will be published here as they are received and approved*

### Categories

- **Recovery journeys**: Stories of improvement and hope
- **Treatment experiences**: What helped and what didn't
- **Living with mental health**: Ongoing management
- **Interactions with healthcare**: GP and service experiences

## Share Your Story

Your experience can help others and improve care.

→ [Submit your story](submit-story.md)

**All stories:**
- Anonymized to protect privacy
- Reviewed and approved by you before use
- Used only with your permission
- Handled with care and respect

## Using These Stories

### For Healthcare Professionals

These stories are used in:
- Medical education
- GP training
- Faculty development
- Quality improvement

**Always with patient permission and appropriate anonymization**

### For Researchers

Stories contribute to:
- Understanding patient priorities
- Evaluating patient experience
- Improving services
- Informing policy

### For Others Experiencing Mental Health Challenges

**You are not alone.** These stories show:
- Recovery is possible
- Different paths work for different people
- Others understand what you're going through
- Help is available

## Safeguarding

**Before reading:**
- Consider if you're in the right headspace
- Have support available if needed
- Take breaks if stories are triggering
- Reach out if you need help

**Support resources:**
- Samaritans: 116 123 (24/7)
- Mind: 0300 123 3393
- NHS Crisis: 111 (option 2)
