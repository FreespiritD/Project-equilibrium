# Share Your Story

## Why Stories Matter

Personal experiences of depression, anxiety, and their treatment help us:
- Understand what matters most to patients
- Improve how we educate future doctors
- Design better clinical pathways
- Inspire others seeking help
- Advocate for better mental health care

**Your story can make a difference.**

## What We're Looking For

### Types of Stories

**Treatment experiences:**
- What treatments you've tried
- What helped and what didn't
- Your treatment preferences
- Decision-making experiences

**Interactions with healthcare:**
- GP consultations
- Referrals and access to services
- Communication and shared decision-making
- What you wished was different

**Journey to recovery:**
- What helped you recover or manage better
- Lifestyle changes that made a difference
- Support from others
- Ongoing management

**What you want future doctors to know:**
- What should medical students learn?
- What do you wish your doctor understood?
- What would improve care?

## How Your Story Will Be Used

### With Your Permission

**Education:**
- Anonymized case studies in medical curriculum
- Teaching materials for GPs
- Faculty development

**Research:**
- Understanding patient priorities
- Identifying gaps in care
- Evaluating patient experience

**Advocacy:**
- Policy briefings (anonymized)
- Conference presentations
- Publications and reports
- Website and communications (optional)

**You control:**
- How much detail you share
- Whether your story is anonymized
- What your story can be used for
- Whether you want to be contacted

## Sharing Your Story

### Option 1: Written Story

**Online form**: [Submit written story](#)

**Guidance:**
- 500-2000 words
- Write freely in your own words
- We can help edit for clarity (with your approval)
- Include as much or as little detail as you wish

**Prompts to help:**
- What led you to seek help?
- What was your experience with treatment?
- What helped? What didn't?
- What do you wish had been different?
- What would you tell medical students?
- What advice would you give to others?

### Option 2: Interview

**Phone or video interview**: [Request interview](#)

- 30-60 minutes
- Conversational and informal
- Recorded with your permission
- Transcribed and sent to you for approval

### Option 3: Anonymous Submission

If you prefer complete anonymity:
- Use anonymous form
- No identifying details
- No contact from us
- Still valuable contribution

## Safeguarding & Support

**Your wellbeing is our priority.**

**Before sharing:**
- Consider whether you're in a good place to reflect
- Have support available if needed
- Take breaks if it becomes difficult
- No pressure to share what's uncomfortable

**During process:**
- Skip any questions
- Stop at any time
- Request support if upset
- Choose anonymous option if preferred

**After sharing:**
- Support resources provided
- Opportunity to withdraw (within 2 weeks)
- Check-in offered (if not anonymous)

**Support resources:**
- Samaritans: 116 123 (24/7)
- Mind: 0300 123 3393

## Consent & Privacy

### What We Ask Permission For

**Essential** (for any use of your story):
- Use your story in EQUILIBRIUM project
- Anonymize your personal details
- Edit for clarity (with your approval of changes)

**Optional** (you choose):
- Use story in public communications (website, reports)
- Use your first name or initials (not full name)
- Contact you for follow-up questions
- Include you in patient advisory group
- Use quotes in publications
- Photo (optional, can be generic/stock photo)

### Privacy Protection

**All stories:**
- Anonymized (names, locations, specific dates removed)
- Stored securely
- Accessible only to approved team members
- Not shared outside project without permission

**Your rights:**
- See what we've written based on your story
- Request changes
- Withdraw consent (within reasonable timeframe)
- Request deletion (within 2 weeks of submission)

## What Happens Next?

1. **Submit your story** via form or interview

## FAQs

**Q: Do I have to use my real name?**
A: No. You can submit completely anonymously or use a pseudonym.

**Q: Will my GP or doctor see my story?**
A: Not unless you specifically request it. All stories are anonymized.

**Q: What if I change my mind?**
A: You can withdraw consent and request deletion within 2 weeks of submission.

**Q: Can I submit someone else's story (family member)?**
A: Yes, with their knowledge and consent. We also welcome carer perspectives.

**Q: What if my story is critical of healthcare?**
A: Constructive criticism helps us improve. We welcome honest experiences.

**Q: How long does this take?**
A: Written: as long as you need. Interview: 30-60 minutes.

**Q: Will I get paid?**
A: We can offer £25 honorarium for interview participation (optional to accept).
