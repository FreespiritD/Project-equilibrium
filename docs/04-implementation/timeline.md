## Key Decision Points (Go/No-Go Gates)

### Gate 1: End of Year 1 (December 2025)
**Decision**: Proceed to pilot implementation?
**Criteria**:
- Pilot sites confirmed
- Curriculum and pathways developed
- Training programs ready
- Funding secured for Years 2-4
- Stakeholder support demonstrated

### Gate 2: Mid-Year 3 (June 2027)
**Decision**: Continue pilot or pivot?
**Criteria**:
- Implementation feasibility demonstrated
- Early indicators positive
- Stakeholder satisfaction adequate
- No major safety or quality concerns
- Funding for scale-up secured or highly likely

### Gate 3: End of Year 4 (December 2028)
**Decision**: Proceed to national scale-up?
**Criteria**:
- Pilot evaluation demonstrates effectiveness
- Cost-effectiveness established
- Graduate competency achieved
- Patient outcomes improved
- System support secured
- Political/policy environment favorable

### Gate 4: End of Year 7 (December 2031)
**Decision**: Continue to full sustainability?
**Criteria**:
- Scale-up successful
- Population-level impact emerging
- Policy integration achieved
- Long-term funding secured
- Broad stakeholder support
