## Evidence Summaries for Policymakers

### Key Statistics

- **23% of UK population** prescribed antidepressants
- **Only 33%** of prescriptions guideline-consistent
- **50%** of patients discontinue prematurely
- **Disability from depression increasing** despite rising prescribing

### Evidence for Alternatives

- **Exercise as effective as medication** for mild-moderate depression
- **CBT comparable outcomes** to medication, better long-term
- **Cost-effective**: £5000-15000 per QALY
- **Patient preference**: 75% prefer non-medication if equally effective

### Return on Investment

- **Implementation cost**: £25-30M over 10 years
- **Expected ROI**: 3:1 (£3 returned per £1 invested)
- **Savings**: £200M/year from reduced prescribing, £500M/year from improved outcomes
- **Benefit-cost ratio**: Highly favorable
