# Research Collaboration Opportunities

## About EQUILIBRIUM Research

Project EQUILIBRIUM involves extensive research across medical education, clinical practice, implementation science, and health economics. We welcome research collaborations!

## Research Themes

### 1. Medical Education Research

**Topics:**
- Curriculum effectiveness and student outcomes
- Assessment of competency development
- Faculty development impacts
- Long-term effects on practice patterns
- Educational innovation evaluation

**Methods:** RCTs, quasi-experimental designs, surveys, qualitative research

### 2. Clinical Implementation Research

**Topics:**
- Clinical pathway effectiveness
- Implementation fidelity and adaptations
- Prescribing pattern changes
- Patient outcomes (depression, anxiety severity, quality of life)
- Health service utilization

**Methods:** Pragmatic trials, observational studies, mixed methods

### 3. Health Economics

**Topics:**
- Cost-effectiveness of holistic approaches
- Return on investment analysis
- Healthcare utilization and costs
- Quality-adjusted life years (QALYs)
- Budget impact modeling

**Methods:** Economic evaluation, modeling, cost-utility analysis

### 4. Implementation Science

**Topics:**
- Barriers and facilitators to adoption
- Strategies for successful implementation
- Sustainability factors
- Scaling-up approaches
- Context-mechanism-outcome patterns

**Methods:** Realist evaluation, process evaluation, case studies

### 5. Patient Experience

**Topics:**
- Patient preferences for treatment
- Shared decision-making processes
- Patient-reported outcomes
- Engagement and adherence
- Equity and access

**Methods:** Surveys, interviews, focus groups, patient-reported outcome measures

## Collaboration Opportunities

### Join Existing Research

**Multi-site studies**: Contribute data or participate in ongoing studies
**Secondary analysis**: Access aggregated data for secondary research questions
**Sub-studies**: Propose add-on studies at pilot sites

### Propose New Research

**Research grants**: Collaborate on funding applications
**PhD/postdoc projects**: Embed research within EQUILIBRIUM
**Student projects**: Supervised medical student research

### Share Expertise

**Methods expertise**: Advise on study design and analysis
**Subject matter expertise**: Guide research questions and interpretation
**Evaluation expertise**: Support evaluation framework

## How to Get Involved

### Step 1: Explore

Review existing research:
- [Research protocols](../protocols/README.md)
- [Publications](../publications/README.md)
- [Data collection methods](../data-collection/README.md)

### Step 2: Express Interest

Complete [collaboration expression of interest](#)

Include:
- Your background and expertise
- Research interests relevant to EQUILIBRIUM
- Type of collaboration sought
- Potential contribution

### Step 3: Discuss

- Phone/video consultation with research lead
- Explore specific opportunities
- Discuss data access, authorship, resources

### Step 4: Formalize

- Research collaboration agreement
- Ethics approval (if needed)
- Data sharing agreement
- Authorship agreement
