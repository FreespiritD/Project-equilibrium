# Project EQUILIBRIUM Governance

## Governance Structure

### Steering Committee (15 members)

**Role:** Strategic direction, oversight, major decisions

**Composition:**
- Chair (independent academic, no pharma ties)
- 3 medical education experts
- 2 psychiatrists (1 integrative medicine)
- 2 GPs (currently practicing)
- 2 medical students (rotating 2-year terms)
- 1 health economist
- 1 patient advocate
- 1 clinical pharmacist
- 1 psychologist
- 1 public health expert
- 1 NHS commissioning representative

**Meeting Frequency:** Quarterly (plus emergency meetings as needed)

**Term Limits:** 3 years (renewable once)

**Decision-Making:** Consensus preferred, majority vote (60%) if needed

**Conflict of Interest Policy:**
- No pharmaceutical industry funding >£10,000
- Annual disclosure required
- Recusal from relevant decisions

### Working Groups (5 groups)

**1. Curriculum Development Working Group**
- Lead: Medical education expert
- Members: 12 (educators, students, clinicians)
- Meets: Monthly
- Deliverables: Curriculum materials, assessment frameworks

**2. Clinical Practice Integration Working Group**
- Lead: Senior GP
- Members: 15 (GPs, practice managers, pharmacists)
- Meets: Monthly
- Deliverables: Clinical pathways, payment models

**3. Research & Evaluation Working Group**
- Lead: Health services researcher
- Members: 10 (researchers, statisticians, economists)
- Meets: Monthly
- Deliverables: Research protocols, evaluation reports

**4. Stakeholder Engagement Working Group**
- Lead: Communications expert
- Members: 12 (patients, advocates, media specialists)
- Meets: Monthly
- Deliverables: Communication strategies, public materials

**5. Implementation Science Working Group**
- Lead: Implementation scientist
- Members: 10 (change management experts, QI specialists)
- Meets: Monthly
- Deliverables: Implementation guides, barrier analysis

### Project Management Team

**Medical Education Lead** (0.8 FTE)

**Research Manager** (1.0 FTE)

**Communications Manager** (0.6 FTE)

## Decision-Making Framework

### Strategic Decisions (Steering Committee)
- Project direction and scope
- Budget allocation
- Pilot site selection
- Scale-up decisions
- Major partnerships

**Process:**
1. Proposal developed by working group or project team
2. Circulated to Steering Committee (2 weeks advance)
3. Discussion at quarterly meeting
4. Decision by consensus or vote
5. Minutes published

### Operational Decisions (Working Groups)
- Curriculum content
- Clinical pathway details
- Research methods
- Implementation strategies

**Process:**
1. Working group discusses and decides
2. Reports to Steering Committee quarterly
3. Steering Committee can veto (rarely used)

### Day-to-Day Decisions (Project Team)
- Scheduling
- Vendor selection (within budget)
- Participant recruitment
- Communication timing

**Process:**
1. Project Director decides
2. Reports to Steering Committee quarterly

## Transparency and Accountability

### Open by Default

**Public:**
- All documentation (except personal data)
- Steering Committee meeting minutes
- Evaluation data (anonymized)
- Budget overview
- Publications

**Available on request:**
- Detailed financial data
- Individual site data
- Working group minutes

**Confidential:**
- Personal participant data
- Commercial contracts (until finalized)
- Personnel matters

### Reporting

**Monthly:**
- Internal progress report to Steering Committee

**Quarterly:**
- Public progress report on website
- Financial summary
- KPI dashboard

**Annually:**
- Comprehensive evaluation report
- Academic publications
- Public annual report
- Audited financial statements

## Conflict Resolution

### Process

**Level 1: Direct Discussion**
- Parties discuss directly
- Working group chair mediates if needed

**Level 2: Project Director**
- Escalated to Project Director
- Decision within 2 weeks

**Level 3: Steering Committee**
- Escalated to Steering Committee
- Discussed at next meeting (or emergency meeting)
- Decision final

### Appeals
- Can appeal Steering Committee decision once
- Must present new information
- Independent reviewer (appointed by Chair) provides recommendation
- Steering Committee makes final decision

## Amendment Process

**This governance document can be amended:**
1. Proposal by any Steering Committee member or working group
2. Discussion period (4 weeks minimum)
3. Vote at Steering Committee meeting
4. Requires 75% approval
5. Published immediately upon approval

## Code of Conduct Enforcement

**See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for full code.**

**Enforcement:**
- Reports to conduct@projectequilibrium.org
- Reviewed by Conduct Committee (3 Steering Committee members)
- Investigation within 2 weeks
- Decision communicated to reporter and alleged violator
- Appeals to full Steering Committee

**Possible actions:**
- Warning
- Temporary suspension from project activities
- Permanent removal from project
- Revocation of access to resources
