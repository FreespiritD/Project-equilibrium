## Mission

To fundamentally restructure medical education and primary care practice to balance pharmaceutical and holistic evidence-based interventions, creating a new generation of physicians equipped to address the root causes of mental health conditions.

## The Problem

- 23% of UK population prescribed antidepressants, yet disability from depression increasing
- Only 33% of SSRI prescriptions consistent with guidelines
- 50% of patients discontinue prematurely
- GPs feel they have no alternatives to prescribing
- Robust evidence for lifestyle/psychological interventions underutilized

## The Solution

**10-year comprehensive transformation:**
- Restructure medical education to include holistic evidence-based approaches
- Reform primary care pathways and payment models
- Train current practitioners in full range of interventions
- Rigorous evaluation proving effectiveness and cost-savings

## Target Outcomes (Year 10)

- 50% reduction in inappropriate antidepressant prescribing
- 40% improvement in long-term mental health outcomes
- 30% reduction in mental health-related healthcare costs
- 80% of new medical graduates competent in holistic intervention delivery
