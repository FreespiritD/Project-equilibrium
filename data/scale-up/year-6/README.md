# Year 6 Scale-Up Data (2023-2024)

**Period:** 2023-2024  
**Phase:** Initial scale-up from 2 medical schools + 8 practices → 5 medical schools + 25 practices

---

## Expected Data

- `new-site-characteristics.csv`: Characteristics of 17 new practices and 3 new medical schools
- `practice-outcomes-year6.csv`: Outcomes from all 25 practices (8 pilot + 17 new)
- `implementation-fidelity.csv`: Assess if new sites achieve similar outcomes to pilot sites
- `scale-up-challenges.csv`: Barriers encountered during rapid expansion
- `training-delivery-log.csv`: Record of all training courses delivered (GP 2-day, staff workshops, online modules)

---

## Key Questions

1. **Replicability:** Do new sites achieve similar outcomes to pilot sites?
2. **Training effectiveness:** Is regional faculty delivering training to same standard as original team?
3. **Hub-and-spoke model:** Are pilot practices effectively mentoring new practices?
