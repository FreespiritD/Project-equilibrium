# Year 4 Pilot Phase Data (2021-2022)

**Academic Year:** 2021-2022  
**Focus:** Continued implementation at pilot sites with refinements based on Year 3 learning

---

## Expected Data Files

### Medical School Data
- `student-knowledge-scores-year4.csv`: Pre-post knowledge (Year 4 students)
- `student-confidence-year4.csv`: Confidence ratings
- `osce-scores-year4.csv`: OSCE assessments (more schools now assessing EQUILIBRIUM)
- `clinical-placement-outcomes.csv`: Student performance in GP/psychiatry placements
  - Variables: student_id, placement_type, depression_cases_seen, guideline_concordance_placement

### GP Practice Data
- `practice-outcomes-quarters-year4.csv`: Quarterly practice outcomes (Q1-Q4)
- `patient-outcomes-year4.csv`: Patient-level outcomes (n=~1,500, increased engagement)
- `prescribing-data-year4.csv`: Practice prescribing patterns
- `implementation-fidelity-audit.csv`: Chart review of biopsychosocial assessment quality
  - Audit of 30 cases per practice, assess % meeting EQUILIBRIUM standards

### Qualitative
- `patient-interviews-year4.csv`: Patient experiences (n=40, 5 per practice)
- `practice-manager-interviews.csv`: Staff perspective on implementation

---

## Key Changes from Year 3

1. **Implementation maturity:** Practices more confident, workflows smoother
2. **Medical school integration:** EQUILIBRIUM now embedded in Year 3 AND Year 4 curriculum at Manchester
3. **Data collection improvements:** Better PHQ-9 completion rates (target 80%+)
4. **Refinements:** Based on Year 3 learning, adjusted training, EHR templates, patient resources

---

## Expected Outcomes - Year 4

### GP Practices
- **PHQ-9 response rate:** 55-62% (continued improvement as implementation matures)
- **Guideline concordance:** 60-70% (larger gains expected)
- **Sustainability:** Assess if practices maintaining fidelity without intensive support

### Medical Schools
- **Knowledge gains:** Consistent with Year 3 (+15-25%)
- **OSCE pass rates:** 75-85% (improved as faculty become more experienced assessing)
- **Student placement performance:** 50-60% guideline-concordant prescribing (vs 29% baseline)

---

*Year 4 represents consolidation and refinement phase. Expect more robust outcomes data and early signals of sustainability.*
