# Year 5 Pilot Phase Data (2022-2023)

**Academic Year:** 2022-2023  
**Focus:** Final pilot year before scale-up; assessment of sustained implementation and outcomes

---

## Expected Data Files

### Medical School Data
- `student-outcomes-year5.csv`: Combined knowledge, confidence, OSCE scores
- `longitudinal-cohort.csv`: Students who completed EQUILIBRIUM in Years 3-5 (Manchester cohort)
- `final-year-performance.csv`: Final year assessments, prescribing competence

### GP Practice Data
- `practice-outcomes-year5.csv`: Annual summary outcomes
- `patient-outcomes-year5.csv`: Patient-level data (n=~1,800)
- `sustainability-assessment.csv`: Implementation sustainability scores (0-100 scale)
- `cost-effectiveness-data.csv`: Costs, savings, QALYs for economic analysis

### Qualitative
- `focus-groups-year5.csv`: GP focus group participants and themes
- `implementation-case-studies/`: Detailed narratives from each practice

---

## Key Outcomes - Year 5 (Target)

### GP Practices (Mature Implementation)
- **PHQ-9 response rate:** 60-67% (approaching best-practice benchmarks)
- **Guideline concordance:** 70-78% (most practices achieving target <30% medication first-line for mild depression)
- **Sustainability:** 6/8 practices maintaining high fidelity without ongoing support

### Medical Schools
- **Full curriculum implementation** (Manchester: Years 3-5)
- **Student competence:** 75-85% placement guideline concordance
- **National comparison:** EQUILIBRIUM students outperform non-EQUILIBRIUM peers on depression management OSCEs

---

## Pilot Phase Summary (Years 3-5)

**Overall trajectory:**
- **Year 3:** Implementation and learning (modest gains)
- **Year 4:** Consolidation (stronger gains)
- **Year 5:** Mature implementation (target outcomes achieved)

**Decision point:** Based on Year 5 data, determine readiness for regional scale-up.
